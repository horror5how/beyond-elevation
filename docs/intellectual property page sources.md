## Claims deliberately NOT made on this page

- "5x your valuation" and "12x chances of closing funding", which appear elsewhere on the site, have no primary source and are not repeated here.
- No claim is made that IP causes a valuation multiple. The page states what was measured: revenue per employee, funding odds, exit odds and growth odds.
- Legal statements are kept general (first to file, absolute novelty, US grace period) and the page carries the existing not-a-law-firm disclaimer.
