FAQ

Every question, answered straight.

How much does a fractional executive cost?

A fraction of one senior full time hire. Fractional starts from $5,800 per month and projects start from $30k, with no salary, no equity and no notice period. Smaller companies can start with a two-week AI Audit at a fixed $3,000. The exact shape is agreed on the free call.

Are you a consultancy?

No. Consultancies advise and leave. We take a position inside the company and own an outcome.

Why only three positions?

Because they are the three where an exited operator plus AI beats a full time hire outright. Everything else, hire properly.

What does plus AI actually mean?

AI does the repetitive work: reading, drafting, monitoring. The operator makes the decisions and answers for the results.

Do you replace my team?

No. We take the work nobody wanted off them. Same people, higher value work.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Code, agents, accounts, documentation. No lock in by design.

How fast does it start?

In the position by week one. First system live by week four.

Do we have to change systems?

No. We build on what you already run.

How small is too small?

If you have no revenue and no board, hire a bookkeeper first. We are worth it from roughly Series A.

Is the free call a sales pitch?

No. Thirty minutes, you bring the thing that annoys you most, and you leave with a straight answer. If there is nothing worth doing, we say so on the call.

What happens after I book?

You get an instant calendar confirmation. No prep needed. Come as you are and we do the digging on the call.

Which process should we automate first?

High volume, rule based, already measured. Anything judgement heavy waits until one agent is live.

Is our data safe with AI in the loop?

Everything runs inside your own accounts and your own stack. Nothing leaves your control, and every agent output is checked before it touches a real number.

Are you a law firm?

No. On the IP side we do not file or prosecute. We decide what is worth owning and what it should earn, then bring counsel in to execute.

Where do you operate?

New York, London and Dubai, with clients across the US, UK, Europe and the Gulf.
