FAQ

Questions.

How much does it cost?

A fraction of one senior full-time hire. Fractional starts from $5,800 per month, projects start from $30k, with no salary, no equity and no notice period. Smaller companies can start with a two-week AI Audit at a fixed $3,000. The exact shape is agreed on the free call.

What is a forward deployed engineer, an FDE?

An engineer who moves into your business and makes the AI work in your world, instead of handing you a roadmap and leaving. Ours are ex C-suite operators with at least one exit behind them. They build it, run it and answer for the results.

One person, really?

One operator with AI covers what used to take a small team. You get a single accountable name instead of a partner, a project manager and two juniors.

Do they replace my team?

No. They take the work nobody wanted off them. Same people, higher value work.

Which process gets automated first?

High volume, rule based, already measured. Anything judgement heavy waits until one agent is live.

Who owns what gets built?

You do. Code, agents, accounts, documentation. No lock-in by design.

How does it roll out across a growing team?

One function at a time behind a named owner, never company wide behind a memo.
