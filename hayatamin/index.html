What he provides

The finance seat and the strategy seat, held by one person.

Financial ownership and control through to the raise or the exit, and the company-wide plan that decides where the money goes. Both jobs, same desk.

01 · Chief Financial Officer

Finance

Old-school work ethic, new-school technology. The numbers hold up when a stranger opens them.

  • Driver-based modelling, unit economics and pricing
  • Budgeting, forecasting and scenario planning
  • Monthly close and the board reporting package
  • Cash, margin and runway answered live, not three weeks late
  • FP&A with variance analysis leadership acts on
  • Payment economics: interchange, scheme fees, FX and transaction margin
  • M&A diligence, transaction planning, close and post-close integration
  • Fundraise support, investor relations and exit readiness

02 · Chief Strategy Officer

Strategy

Where the money is, what to stop doing today, and the operating rhythm that makes the plan happen.

  • Company-wide strategy and the annual operating plan
  • OKR architecture, QBRs, dashboards and KPI tracking
  • Pricing and the commercial motion into enterprise buyers
  • Market sizing, positioning and the fundraise architecture
  • Buy, partner or build calls, sized against internal capability
  • Data monetisation: from by-product to a priced, licensed asset
  • Board and sponsor relationship, value creation plan tracking
  • Executive storytelling: complex matters into simple recommendations
