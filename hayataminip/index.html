IP Strategy and Valuation

IP strategistValuationPortfolio strategyData assets

Portfolio strategy

  • What the estate protects, what it is worth and where filing spend goes next
  • Filings that defend nothing get dropped; spend goes where licensees build

Claims, not titles

  • Every patent read from claim 1, element by element, before it is valued, mapped or offered

Valuation a buyer can underwrite

  • Patents and data valued as balance sheet assets for buyers and investors
  • The evidence built before anyone asks: claim scope, licensing pipeline, revenue per family

IP and data in the exit price

  • Wrote the proprietary data case for TripAdvisor in the Tripbod sale
