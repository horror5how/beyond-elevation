How much does it cost?

A fraction of one senior full-time hire. Fractional starts from $5,800 per month, projects start from $30k, with no salary, no equity and no notice period. Smaller companies can start with a two-week AI Audit at a fixed $3,000. The exact shape is agreed on the free call.

Are you a consultancy?

No. Consultancies advise and leave. We take a role inside your company and take responsibility for results.

Why only three positions?

They are the three roles where a senior operator plus AI beats a full-time hire outright. Everything else, hire properly.

What does "plus AI" actually mean?

AI does the repetitive work: reading, drafting, monitoring. The operator makes the decisions and answers for the results.

Do you replace my team?

No. We take the work nobody wanted off them. Same people, higher value work.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Code, agents, accounts, documentation. No lock-in by design.
