Forward deployed engineer agency: 8 places to hire an FDE in 2026, compared

Hayat Amin · Updated 2026-11-09

A forward deployed engineer agency puts an engineer inside your company to build AI systems on your real data. Here are 8 real options, from firms that build it for you to talent networks and recruiters, with prices, start times and who each one is wrong for.

A forward deployed engineer agency is a firm that puts an engineer inside your company to build and run AI systems on your real data, then hands them over. The 8 firms below come in 6 kinds: a firm that does the work itself, a talent network, a freelance marketplace, a nearshore or offshore developer, a Big 4 consultancy, and recruiters who find you a full-time hire. The right one depends on whether you need a system built this quarter or a person on payroll for years.

Hayat Amin wrote this. He spent twenty years as a technology CFO, sold three companies, and now works as a forward deployed engineer himself, so he's sat on both sides of this purchase: the one signing the invoice and the one doing the build. Beyond Elevation, his firm, is entry 1 on this list. That's disclosed here, and the other 7 are judged on what their own pages say.

why "forward deployed engineer agency" is a hard search

Type the phrase into Google and most of what comes back is job boards. Our August 2026 research found the same thing: the term autocompletes, but the results are listings for engineers looking for work, not firms selling the work to a CEO. Meanwhile demand keeps climbing. Forward deployed engineer job listings grew about 800% between January and September 2025 and more than 1,000% year on year into early 2026, according to Perspective AI's analysis of 1,000 job posts, Pragmatic Engineer and Paraform.

So a chief executive in New York or Chicago who wants one has to sort 3 different businesses that all use the same label. Some sell you an engineer by the month, some sell you a project, and some find you a hire and leave. The list below runs from the firms that build to the firms that recruit.

how this list was built

I read each firm's own page on 9 November 2026 and only used what it says there: prices, trial terms, time to start, and how it vets people. Where a firm doesn't publish a price, I've said so. Nothing here is ranked by quality. Each entry is placed by the kind of buyer it suits, with who it's wrong for, because that's the decision you're making.

8 forward deployed engineer agencies, compared

1. Beyond Elevation (disclosure: Hayat Amin's firm)

Beyond Elevation does the FDE work itself. Hayat Amin sits inside the company, connects the systems, builds the agents and the dashboards, and hands them over. Its prices are published on beyondelevation.com/fde: a two week AI audit is $3,000 fixed and ends with a roadmap and your three fastest wins, fractional work at 1 to 2 days a week starts from $5,800 a month with no notice period, and a project of 8 to 14 weeks starts from $30,000. You own the code, the agents, the accounts and the documents. It suits a US company of 20 to 500 people whose CEO wants a senior operator with finance judgment, not a bench of juniors. It's wrong for a company that needs 5 engineers on one rollout next month.

2. A.Team

A.Team supplies senior engineers from its network who embed with your customer and carry your badge. Its page quotes roughly $25,000 a month for a senior builder, fully loaded, with no placement fee, and promises named, priced profiles in 3 to 5 business days. It says a full delivery team can be assembled within a week, and that it scores every builder 0 to 100 across 8 criteria from a bench of 3,000 with under 2% acceptance. At $25,000 a month, a year comes to $300,000. It suits a software company with enterprise customers waiting on rollouts. It's wrong for a 40 person firm that needs 2 days a week.

3. Toptal

Toptal sells freelance forward deployed engineers and says fewer than 3% of applicants are accepted. Matches average under 24 hours and a hire takes about 48 hours. Every engagement starts with a trial of up to 2 weeks, and you aren't billed if you're not satisfied. You can hire hourly, part time or full time. It publishes no rates on its FDE page. It suits a CEO who already knows exactly what has to be built and can manage a freelancer. It's wrong if nobody in the company can write that brief.

4. Fullstack

Fullstack places nearshore FDEs who work in US time zones, with sample profiles in Brazil, Ecuador, Colombia and Honduras. It offers 2 free weeks, and if it isn't working in that time you pay nothing and they swap or stop. It sends a shortlist within 48 hours of the first call and says the cost is roughly half a US hire, but gives no dollar figure. It suits a company with a technical lead who'll manage the engineer day to day. It's wrong if you need someone to sit with your COO and decide what to build.

5. Andersen

Andersen is a development firm headquartered in Warsaw, with development centres in Munich and Krakow and a representative office in London. Its FDE page quotes offshore rates of roughly $40 to $90 an hour, which at 160 hours is $6,400 to $14,400 a month, and fixes a price range after a readiness assessment and scoping call. Most engagements start within 1 to 2 weeks. It suits a cost-led build with a clear specification. It's wrong for work that depends on someone being in the room with your operations team.

6. Deloitte

Deloitte sells forward deployed engineering as a named consulting service, delivered by small, industry-focused pods. Its page sets out milestones of 3 days to a use case, 3 weeks to proven value and 3 months to production. On 12 August 2026 Deloitte said it was the first global system integrator to staff and deliver a Salesforce FDE engagement, as a founding member of Salesforce's FDE Partner Network, building Agentforce solutions. It publishes no prices. It suits a large company already running Salesforce at scale. It's wrong for most companies under 500 people, who'll pay for a structure they don't need.

7. Paraform

Paraform is a recruiting firm, so it finds you a full-time FDE rather than renting you one. Its September 2026 guide puts FDE base salaries at $150,000 to $217,000, with a $183,000 midpoint and staff or principal roles up to $288,000. It charges 25% of first-year base, only after a hire, which on the midpoint is $45,750, and it runs a free replacement search if the hire fails within 90 days. It tells you to budget 8 to 12 weeks for the search. It suits a funded startup ready to build an FDE team. It's wrong if you need the problem solved before the search would finish.

8. Christian & Timbers

Christian & Timbers is a retained executive search firm. Its own 2026 list of FDE recruiters describes its model as retained search with a 12 month guarantee, and says it isn't set up for high-volume individual hires. It doesn't publish its fee. It suits a company hiring a head of forward deployed engineering who'll build a team. It's wrong for a first single hire on a tight budget.

what to do this week, before you call any of them

You can save yourself a wasted month with 5 steps, in this order.

  1. Write down the one process you want fixed first, and the systems it touches: your CRM, your ERP or accounting system, your inbox, the spreadsheet someone updates on Monday.
  2. Time it for 5 working days and put today's cost in dollars on one line. That's the number any agency has to beat.
  3. Decide if you need a build or a hire. If it's one process and 8 to 14 weeks of work, you need a build. If you have 5 or more enterprise customers waiting on rollouts, you need a hire.
  4. Check who holds the admin logins and whether each system has an API or an export. Any agency will ask on the first call.
  5. Put the same written brief in front of 2 firms from different rows above, and compare what they'd ship in the first 30 days.

If the systems connect and you have a technical lead, a marketplace or nearshore firm can take that brief and run. If the data sits in 4 systems that don't talk, nobody owns the process and the board wants a number by next quarter, that's bigger than a weekend fix, and it's when you bring in a forward deployed engineer who'll sit with your leadership. Frequently asked questions

What is a forward deployed engineer agency?

It's a firm that places an engineer inside your company to build AI systems on your real data and hand them over. Some do the work themselves, some rent you engineers by the month, and some recruit a full-time hire for a fee.

How much does it cost to hire a forward deployed engineer through an agency?

From the pages read for this list: about $40 to $90 an hour offshore at Andersen, roughly $25,000 a month for a senior builder at A.Team, from $5,800 a month fractional at Beyond Elevation, or 25% of first-year base salary through Paraform, which is $45,750 on a $183,000 salary.

Should I hire a forward deployed engineer full time or through an agency?

Hire full time when you have 5 or more enterprise customers waiting on rollouts and 8 to 12 weeks to run a search. Use an agency or a fractional engineer when one process needs fixing this quarter.

How fast can an FDE agency start?

Toptal says about 48 hours, Fullstack sends a shortlist within 48 hours, A.Team sends profiles in 3 to 5 business days and Andersen usually starts within 1 to 2 weeks. A recruited full-time hire takes 8 to 12 weeks, by Paraform's estimate.
