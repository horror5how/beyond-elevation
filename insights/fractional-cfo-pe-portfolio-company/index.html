Fractional CFO for PE Portfolio Companies: The Sponsor's Finance Audit Your Controller Will Fail

Hayat Amin · Updated 2026-11-18

PE sponsors run a 12-point finance diagnostic within 100 days of close. A fractional CFO for PE portfolio companies builds the reporting stack that passes it, at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire gone wrong.

PE sponsors run a finance diagnostic within 100 days of every acquisition. According to Bain and Company's 2025 Global Private Equity Report, 64% of portfolio companies undergo a finance leadership change within 18 months of close. The number is not about bad people. It is about mismatched infrastructure.

Hayat Amin reminds founders that a sponsor measures your finance function against a 12-point checklist the day the deal closes, not six months later. The controller who ran the business for five years built it for the founder. The sponsor needs a finance function built for a board, a reporting deadline, and an exit. Those are three different audiences with three different reporting standards.

A fractional CFO for PE portfolio companies closes that structural gap in 90 days at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire gone wrong. Beyond Elevation places operators who have sat on both sides of the sponsor table into portfolio companies where the finance function needs rebuilding against a live clock.

What Does a PE Sponsor Measure in a Portfolio Company Finance Function?

PE sponsors measure six outputs in the first 100 days: cash visibility, covenant compliance, management reporting, audit readiness, working capital efficiency, and value-creation tracking against the investment thesis. Portfolio companies that fail on more than two of these face a finance leadership replacement within the first quarter. This is not a subjective assessment. It is a checklist the deal team builds before close and hands to the operating partner on day one.

The sponsor does not want a monthly P&L delivered three weeks late. They want a weekly cash position report every Monday, a 13-week rolling cash flow forecast updated every Friday, a covenant compliance certificate delivered to the facility provider on schedule, and a board pack that maps operational KPIs to the agreed value-creation plan.

Most controllers have never built a 13-week forecast. Most have never prepared a covenant compliance certificate. The gap is structural, not personal, and it is the reason PE sponsors hire fractional CFOs to bridge the first 100 days.

Why Does a Fractional CFO Work Better Than a Full-Time Hire for PE Portfolio Companies?

A fractional CFO delivers sponsor-grade finance infrastructure at 20 to 30% of the cost of a full-time CFO, with zero ramp-up time and no severance risk if the engagement scope changes after close. The economics are direct: a full-time PE-calibre CFO costs between 150,000 and 250,000 pounds in base salary plus bonus, takes three to six months to recruit, and needs another three months to deliver meaningful output. A fractional CFO for PE portfolio companies starts in week one and delivers the first board pack by week four.

Hayat Amin's view is blunt: sponsors do not have six months to wait for a finance hire to find the building. The covenant report is due in 45 days. The reporting cycle starts in 30. You need someone who has built this reporting stack before, not someone learning on the company's budget.

The fractional model also eliminates the single biggest risk in PE finance leadership: hiring a CFO who built their career in corporate and has never reported to a sponsor. That mismatch drives the high replacement rate. A fractional CFO from an operator background understands what the quarterly business review is designed to catch because they have presented at one. What Does Hayat Amin's PE Portfolio Finance Stack Look Like?

Hayat Amin's PE Portfolio Finance Stack is the 90-day build sequence Beyond Elevation deploys into every portfolio company engagement. It has four layers, built in strict order, with each layer delivering a live output the sponsor can review within the same week it is built. No layer depends on a system migration, an ERP implementation, or a consulting engagement.

Layer 1, Days 1 to 14: Cash Architecture. Build the 13-week rolling cash flow forecast, reconcile all bank accounts, and map every cash inflow and outflow to a named category. The deliverable is a weekly one-page cash dashboard the sponsor reviews every Monday morning.

Layer 2, Days 15 to 30: Covenant and Reporting Compliance. Map every facility agreement covenant to a live tracker, set automated threshold alerts at 80% and 90% of each limit, and build the compliance certificate template. The deliverable is the first covenant compliance certificate, delivered on time.

Layer 3, Days 31 to 60: Management Reporting and Board Pack. Design the monthly management accounts with the sponsor's preferred KPI set. Build the EBITDA bridge from reported to adjusted, with every add-back documented and justified. Create the board pack template tied to the value-creation plan. The deliverable is the first sponsor-grade board pack.

Layer 4, Days 61 to 90: Working Capital and Value Creation. Implement debtor days, creditor days, and inventory cycle tracking. Map working capital movements to cash conversion. Build the value-creation waterfall that tracks each initiative against the 100-day plan. The deliverable is the first quarterly business review deck.

The entire stack is built on the existing tech stack. Spreadsheets, cloud accounting tools, and the systems already in place. This is what the first 100 days of a fractional CFO engagement looks like when the reporting audience is a PE sponsor rather than a founder.

How Is a PE Portfolio Fractional CFO Different From a Startup Fractional CFO?

A PE portfolio fractional CFO reports to a sponsor, manages facility provider relationships, and tracks value creation against an investment thesis. A startup fractional CFO reports to a founder and builds toward a fundraise. The skill sets overlap at roughly 40%, and the gap in the other 60% causes expensive hiring mistakes in PE-backed businesses.

Three differences matter most.

First, covenant compliance. Startup CFOs rarely manage debt covenants. PE portfolio CFOs manage them weekly and understand that a technical breach triggers cross-default provisions that can accelerate the entire facility.

Second, EBITDA adjustments. Startup CFOs report revenue and burn rate. PE portfolio CFOs build and defend an EBITDA bridge where every add-back is documented, justified, and auditable. The exit multiple is applied to that adjusted number, so a poorly documented bridge costs real money at exit.

Third, working capital management. Startup CFOs focus on runway. PE portfolio CFOs focus on cash conversion, because the sponsor's return depends on how much cash the business generates between acquisition and exit, not how long it survives.

Hayat Amin proved this distinction in a portfolio restructuring where the existing finance team had clean monthly accounts but no 13-week forecast, no covenant tracker, and no EBITDA bridge. The numbers were accurate. They were also useless to the sponsor. Accuracy without structure is expensive in PE finance.

When Should a PE Portfolio Company Move From Fractional to Full-Time CFO?

The transition point sits at roughly 15 to 25 million pounds in revenue, three or more reporting entities, or the beginning of active exit preparation. Below that threshold, a fractional CFO delivers the same output quality at lower cost and lower risk.

Hayat Amin argues the question is usually asked backwards: sponsors ask when to hire a full-time CFO when they should ask whether the finance function is built well enough for a full-time CFO to run it. Hiring full-time into a broken structure gives someone a 200,000-pound salary to rebuild what a fractional should have built first.

FAQ

How much does a fractional CFO cost for a PE portfolio company?

Fractional CFO rates for PE portfolio companies typically range from 4,000 to 10,000 pounds per month depending on complexity, number of reporting entities, and facility obligations. This compares to 150,000 to 250,000 pounds annual cost for a full-time hire before recruitment fees and bonus.

Can a fractional CFO handle covenant compliance reporting?

Yes. Covenant compliance is a core deliverable of any PE-experienced fractional CFO. The engagement includes building the covenant tracker, setting automated threshold alerts, and delivering the compliance certificate on schedule every reporting period.

How fast can a fractional CFO build sponsor-grade reporting?

Using the PE Portfolio Finance Stack framework, the first cash dashboard ships in week two, the first covenant certificate by day 30, and the full board pack by day 60. The 90-day sequence delivers usable outputs from the first week.

What qualifications should a PE portfolio fractional CFO have?
