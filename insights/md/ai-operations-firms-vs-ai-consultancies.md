# AI Consultancies vs AI Operations Firms: Who Actually Ships

Seventy percent of AI projects never make it past the pilot stage. For most companies, the problem is not the technology — it is who they hired to deploy it.

An AI operations firm embeds hands-on operators inside your business to build, deploy, and run AI systems. An AI consultancy delivers strategy decks, maturity assessments, and recommendations. The difference is execution. If your AI initiative stalled after the strategy phase, you hired the wrong type of firm.

According to RAND Corporation's 2024 research, over 80 percent of AI projects fail — a rate twice that of traditional IT projects. Hayat Amin argues that the failure rate is not an AI problem — it is a hiring problem. Most companies buy advice when they need an operator. The distinction between AI consultancies and [AI operations firms](/insights/what-is-ai-operations/) determines whether your AI investment produces working systems or a shelf full of strategy documents nobody reads.

## What Is the Difference Between an AI Consultancy and an AI Operations Firm?

An AI consultancy assesses your readiness, designs a strategy, and hands you a roadmap. An AI operations firm takes that roadmap — or builds one — and executes it inside your business. The consultancy leaves when the deck is delivered. The operations firm stays until the system runs without them.

Most AI consultancies operate on a project basis. A typical engagement runs 8 to 16 weeks, involves a team of analysts and senior partners, and produces a strategy document, vendor evaluation, and implementation roadmap. The consultancy identifies what you should do. It does not do it.

AI operations firms work differently. They embed a fractional or full-time operator — someone who has built and shipped AI systems before — directly into your team. That operator configures tools, connects data pipelines, trains internal staff, and deploys production-ready AI workflows. The deliverable is not a document. It is a working system.

Hayat Amin's rule for distinguishing the two is straightforward: ask the firm what happens to your AI system after the engagement ends. If the answer is "you will need to hire someone to implement our recommendations," you are talking to a consultancy. If the answer is "your team will run it because we trained them during the build," you are talking to an operations firm.

## How Much Do AI Operations Firms and AI Consultancies Cost?

AI consultancies typically charge between £150,000 and £500,000 for a strategy engagement, plus implementation costs that often double the total spend. AI operations firms charge fractional retainers between £5,000 and £15,000 per month, delivering both strategy and execution in one fee. The cost gap is not marginal — it is structural.

## When Should You Hire an AI Consultancy?

AI consultancies earn their fee in three situations: when you need board-level AI strategy documentation for investor or regulatory purposes, when you need a vendor-neutral technology assessment before a major platform decision, or when regulatory compliance requires an independent third-party review of your AI governance framework.

If your board wants an independent AI readiness assessment before approving a seven-figure technology budget, a consultancy provides the credibility and structure that internal teams cannot. If you are navigating the EU AI Act and need a formal risk classification before August 2026, a consultancy with regulatory expertise is the right call.

The consultancy model breaks down when you need execution. A strategy deck does not configure your data pipelines. A maturity assessment does not train your finance team to use AI-powered reporting. Recommendations do not ship. That gap is where AI operations firms exist.

## When Should You Hire an AI Operations Firm?

An AI operations firm is the right hire when your company is ready to deploy AI but lacks the internal expertise to execute. This describes the majority of mid-market companies — they know AI should be transforming their operations, but they do not have an AI-experienced operator on staff to make it happen.

Five signals you need an AI operations firm, not a consultancy. You already have an AI strategy but nobody to execute it. You have tested AI tools in isolation but cannot integrate them into existing workflows. Your team spends more time evaluating vendors than using AI. You hired a data scientist who built models that never reached production. You tried a consultancy engagement that produced a roadmap your team could not follow.

Hayat Amin calls this the "strategy trap" — companies that cycle through consultancy engagements without ever deploying a single production AI system. Each engagement produces a new deck. Each deck sits on a shelf. The company falls further behind competitors who hired operators instead of advisors.

## What Does a Good AI Operations Firm Actually Deliver?

A good AI operations firm delivers measurable outcomes within 90 days: automated workflows that replace manual processes, integrated AI tools your team uses daily, and documented systems that survive the operator's departure. The deliverable is never a deck — it is a change in how your business operates.

Hayat Amin's Ship-or-Skip Test is the diagnostic Beyond Elevation runs on every AI operations engagement. It asks five questions. Does the firm assign a named operator or a rotating team? Does the contract include production deployment milestones? Is pricing tied to outcomes or billable hours? Does the operator get direct access to your systems and data? Will your team run the AI systems independently within six months?

If the answer to any of those is no, you are buying advice dressed up as operations. A genuine AI operations firm commits to shipping working systems on a timeline, with your team trained to maintain them after the engagement ends.

## FAQ

### Can an AI consultancy also handle implementation?

Some consultancies offer implementation as a separate engagement, but it is rarely their core competence. The team that wrote your strategy deck is not the team that builds production systems. Implementation is usually subcontracted or staffed with junior engineers, adding cost and reducing accountability. If you need both strategy and execution, an AI operations firm delivers both in one engagement at a lower total cost.

### How long does it take an AI operations firm to show results?

A competent AI operations firm deploys the first production workflow within 30 to 60 days. Measurable ROI — reduced manual hours, faster processing, lower error rates — typically appears within 90 days. Full transformation of a business function takes 6 to 12 months depending on complexity and data readiness.

### Is an AI operations firm right for a company with no AI experience?

Yes — that is precisely the company they serve. AI operations firms are designed for organisations that lack internal AI expertise. The operator brings the technical knowledge, builds the systems, and transfers the capability to your team over time. No prior AI experience is required from the client side.

### What is the difference between an AI operations firm and hiring a full-time AI lead?

A full-time AI lead costs £200,000 to £350,000 per year in total compensation, plus three to six months to recruit. A fractional AI operations engagement costs £60,000 to £180,000 per year and starts within days. The fractional model also gives you access to an operator who has shipped AI across multiple companies and industries, not just one.

### How do I evaluate AI operations firms before hiring?

Ask three questions. First, show me a system you deployed that is still running in production. Second, what will my team be able to do independently after you leave? Third, what is your timeline for the first deployed workflow? Any firm that answers with generalities instead of specifics is selling consulting, not operations.
