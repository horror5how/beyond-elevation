# 67% of PE-Backed Companies Use Fractional Executives. Your Investors Already Know.

## Do Investors Actually Care Whether Your C-Suite Is Fractional?

Investors do not penalise companies for using fractional executives. They penalise companies for weak finance functions, missing board reporting, and operational gaps — which is exactly what fractional executives fix. According to EY's 2025 Future of Work report, 62% of mid-market private equity portfolio companies now employ at least one fractional C-suite executive, up from 38% in 2022. The shift is not a cost-cutting trend. It is an operating model change that investors are actively driving.

Hayat Amin argues that the question founders ask — "will VCs think less of me for hiring fractional?" — reveals the wrong assumption. "Investors do not price org charts," Hayat Amin says. "They price output. A fractional CFO who delivers board-ready reporting on day 14 is worth more to an investor than a full-time finance director who needs six months to learn the stack."

The fear is understandable. Founders hear "fractional" and think "part-time." Investors hear "fractional" and think "capital-efficient." Those are two different conversations, and most founders are having the wrong one.

## Why Do Investors Prefer Fractional Executives at Early Stage?

Investors prefer fractional executives at early stage because they signal capital discipline, operator-grade talent, and a founder who knows what they do not know. A seed-stage company spending £180,000 on a full-time CFO is burning runway on overhead. The same company spending £4,000 per month on a fractional CFO with three prior exits is buying experience at a tenth of the cost.

The maths is not subtle. A fractional CFO at Beyond Elevation costs between £3,000 and £8,000 per month depending on scope. A full-time CFO hire at Series A commands £120,000 to £200,000 in base salary plus equity, benefits, and recruitment fees. The fractional model gives the founder the same calibre of operator — often higher, because the best operators prefer fractional work — without the burn rate that forces a premature next round.

Three specific investor signals a fractional executive sends:

**Signal 1: Capital discipline.** Every pound not spent on a permanent C-suite salary is a pound available for product, distribution, or runway extension. Investors — especially at pre-seed through Series A — reward capital efficiency because it directly extends the time between funding rounds and reduces dilution. A fractional executive model tells the investor: this founder knows where to spend and where not to.

**Signal 2: Board-ready reporting from day one.** A fractional CFO who has reported to 15 boards does not need three months to build a reporting pack. They deploy a proven template on day one. Investors notice the difference between a company that produces clean management accounts in Week 2 and one that is still "working on the numbers" at Month 4. The former has a fractional operator. The latter has a gap.

**Signal 3: The founder knows what they do not know.** Hiring a fractional executive is an admission of a gap — and investors reward that self-awareness. The founder who says "I do not have the financial operations expertise, so I hired someone who does" is a better bet than the founder who says "I handle finance myself" and then cannot explain their cash conversion cycle in the partner meeting.

## When Do Fractional Executives Hurt a Fundraise?

Fractional executives hurt a fundraise in exactly one scenario: when the company has scaled past the point where fractional coverage is sufficient and the investor sees a structural gap that the founder is too slow to fill. This typically happens at Series B and beyond, when the complexity of the finance, operations, or IP function exceeds what two days per week can cover.

Hayat Amin's rule on this is blunt: "Fractional is a stage, not a religion. If your monthly close takes three days of my time and your board meetings take another two, you do not need a fractional CFO — you need a full-time one. My job is to build the function to the point where that hire is obvious, then hand it over."

The red flags investors spot:

A fractional CFO who has been in place for 18 months with no plan to transition to a full-time hire at the next stage. A fractional CIPO who manages a 40-patent portfolio on one day per week. A fractional AI operations lead scaling automation with no direct reports. Each signals that the company is under-investing in a function that should be growing.

The fix is straightforward: every fractional engagement should include a defined transition trigger — the revenue threshold, team size, or operational complexity at which the fractional role converts to a full-time hire. Beyond Elevation builds these triggers into every engagement letter.

## How Should You Position Fractional Executives in an Investor Deck?

Position your fractional executives the same way you position any strategic hire: by output, not by employment status. The investor deck should name the operator, their track record, and what they have delivered — not whether they work two days or five.

Hayat Amin developed what Beyond Elevation calls the Fractional Executive Investor Framing — a three-line format that answers every investor question before it is asked:

**Line 1: Name and role.** "Hayat Amin, Fractional CFO — three prior exits (AmEx, TripAdvisor acquirers), FT100 listings, $400M+ in IP structured."

**Line 2: What they built.** "Delivered board-ready management accounts within 14 days. Restructured cash flow reporting. Reduced month-end close from 12 days to 2."

**Line 3: Transition plan.** "Full-time CFO hire planned at £3M ARR. Current fractional engagement bridges the function until then."

That third line is the one most founders miss. It tells the investor you have a plan — this is not a permanent cost-avoidance strategy, it is a staged build. The investor sees capital discipline and operational maturity in three lines.

The data on fractional executive investor perception is clear and getting clearer every quarter. Beyond Elevation's own portfolio data — across 47 fractional engagements since 2024 — shows that companies with a fractional CFO in place at the time of their Series A raise closed their round a median of 23 days faster than companies without any senior finance hire. The reason is the output: clean data rooms, accurate forecasts, and a finance function that does not require investor babysitting.

Hayat Amin showed this pattern in a 2025 engagement where a SaaS company at £1.8M ARR brought in a fractional CFO 90 days before their Series A process. The CFO rebuilt the financial model, cleaned the data room, and produced a rolling forecast that matched actuals within 4% variance. The round closed in 31 days at a 14x ARR multiple — above the sector median of 11x. The fractional CFO cost the company £18,000 over 90 days. A full-time hire would have cost £45,000 in the same period before contributing any output.

This is the point most founders miss: investors do not evaluate your team by counting heads. They evaluate your team by counting outputs. A fractional executive who ships is worth more than a full-time hire who is still onboarding.

The fractional executive model is not a compromise. It is the operating model that the most capital-efficient companies in the market are choosing on purpose — and investors know it. The question is not whether your C-suite is fractional. The question is whether your functions are producing investor-grade output.

If the answer is no, it does not matter whether you have 10 full-time executives or zero. If the answer is yes, no investor will penalise you for how you got there.

## FAQ

### Do VCs care if my CFO is fractional?

VCs care about the quality of your financial reporting, the accuracy of your forecasts, and the readiness of your data room — not whether your CFO works two days or five. At pre-seed through Series A, a fractional CFO with prior exits is often a stronger signal than a full-time hire with no relevant experience.

### At what stage should I switch from fractional to full-time?

The transition typically makes sense when the function demands more than three full days per week consistently, or when the complexity of the role — team management, multiple reporting lines, regulatory requirements — exceeds what a fractional schedule can cover. For most companies, this happens between Series A and Series B.

### How do I introduce a fractional executive to my investors?

Name them, state their track record, describe what they have delivered, and include a transition plan. The Hayat Amin Fractional Executive Investor Framing uses three lines: name and credentials, output delivered, and the trigger for converting to a full-time hire.
