# You Do Not Need Revenue to Need a Fractional CFO: The Pre-Seed Hire That Closes Rounds Faster

Pre-seed founders hire a fractional CFO because the financial architecture that closes a seed round cannot be built after the term sheet arrives. According to DocSend's 2025 Fundraising Report, startups with investor-ready financial documentation raised 40% faster than those that scrambled to build models mid-process. Hayat Amin argues the fractional CFO conversation starts the day a founder incorporates — not the day they run out of cash.

Most founders wait until revenue hits six figures before they think about finance leadership. By then, the cap table is messy, the tax structure is wrong, and the financial model is a spreadsheet that makes investors nervous. A fractional CFO at pre-seed costs less than a single bad tax election costs to unwind.

## What Does a Fractional CFO Do at Pre-Seed?

A fractional CFO at pre-seed builds the financial infrastructure that makes a company investable before it has generated a single pound of recurring revenue. This is not bookkeeping and it is not tax filing — it is the structural work that determines whether a seed round closes in eight weeks or eight months.

The deliverables are specific: cap table modelling, financial projections, unit economics dashboards, tax-efficient entity design and investor reporting. Beyond Elevation deploys fractional CFOs at pre-seed because the cost of fixing bad financial architecture after a round closes is five to ten times the cost of building it correctly from day one.

The mistake most founders make is treating finance as an operational function. At pre-seed, finance is a fundraising function. Every deliverable your fractional CFO produces is an artefact an investor will request during due diligence. Build it before the ask, and the round moves faster.

## Why Do Pre-Seed Founders Think They Do Not Need a Fractional CFO?

Pre-seed founders skip the fractional CFO hire because they confuse accounting with finance. Accounting records what already happened. Finance builds what needs to happen next. A bookkeeper can file your VAT return. A bookkeeper cannot model the unit economics that convince an investor your business will reach contribution margin by month fourteen.

Hayat Amin's view is direct: "If your accountant is your most senior finance voice, you do not have a finance function. You have a compliance function." The distinction matters because investors price companies on forward-looking financial credibility, not backward-looking transaction records.

Three misconceptions drive the delay:

**1. "We have no revenue, so there is nothing to manage."** A fractional CFO at pre-seed does not manage revenue. They build the financial model, structure the cap table, and design the reporting cadence that makes the company fundable.

**2. "Our accountant handles everything."** Your accountant handles compliance. Strategy, modelling, investor materials and tax-efficient structuring are CFO-grade work that sits outside any accountant's scope.

**3. "We cannot afford it."** A fractional CFO engagement at pre-seed runs £2,000 to £4,000 per month for 15 to 25 hours. One misstructured SEIS round costs more to fix than twelve months of fractional CFO support.

## What Are the Five Pre-Seed Fractional CFO Deliverables?

A fractional CFO at pre-seed produces five deliverables that form the financial backbone of a fundraising process. These are the same outputs a Series A CFO builds, sized and sequenced for a company that has not yet closed its first institutional round.

Hayat Amin developed the **Pre-Seed Finance Diagnostic** — a framework Beyond Elevation runs on every new pre-seed engagement — to identify which of these five deliverables is the binding constraint on a founder's next raise.

**1. Cap Table Model.** A clean cap table with scenario modelling for the seed round, option pool expansion, and convertible note conversion. Most pre-seed cap tables contain at least one structural error that surfaces during due diligence and stalls the round.

**2. Three-Year Financial Model.** Not a revenue forecast built on hope. A bottoms-up model that connects customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, gross margin and burn rate into a coherent story an investor can stress-test against comparable companies in the same sector.

**3. Unit Economics Dashboard.** A one-page view of contribution margin, payback period and gross margin by cohort or channel. This is the page investors turn to after the pitch deck — and the one most pre-seed founders do not have.

**4. Tax-Efficient Entity Structure.** SEIS and EIS eligibility in the UK. R&D tax credit positioning. Holding company versus operating company decisions that become expensive to reverse after a seed round closes. Getting this wrong at pre-seed creates a remediation bill at Series A that dwarfs the original CFO cost.

**5. Investor Reporting Template.** A monthly update format that tracks cash position, burn rate, revenue run rate, pipeline and key milestones. Building this habit at pre-seed creates the reporting muscle that makes post-seed board meetings productive from month one.

A pre-seed fractional CFO engagement costs between £2,000 and £4,000 per month for 15 to 25 hours of work. This covers the five core deliverables, monthly reporting, and ad hoc investor query support during an active raise.

According to Kruze Consulting's 2025 benchmark data, the median full-time CFO hire in a venture-backed startup commands £180,000 to £250,000 in total compensation. A fractional engagement delivers 80% of the financial output at less than 20% of the cost — and avoids the under-utilisation problem that makes a full-time CFO hire at pre-seed a poor allocation of limited capital.

The economics are straightforward. A fractional CFO working two to three days per month builds everything a pre-seed company needs. A full-time hire at this stage would spend 60% of their time without enough work to justify the salary. Hayat Amin reminds founders that the question is not whether you can afford a fractional CFO at pre-seed — it is whether you can afford the fundraising delay that comes from not having one.

## What Do Investors Check at Pre-Seed Before Writing a Cheque?

Investors at pre-seed check three financial signals before they commit capital: cap table cleanliness, financial model coherence, and tax structure eligibility. A fractional CFO ensures all three pass inspection before the first investor meeting — not during it.

Cap table errors are the most common reason pre-seed due diligence stalls. Missing vesting schedules, incorrectly converted notes, or option pools that do not match the articles force legal remediation mid-round. According to Carta's 2025 data, 34% of pre-seed companies had at least one cap table discrepancy that required correction before closing.

Hayat Amin says the investor conversation has shifted permanently: "Five years ago, pre-seed investors accepted a napkin financial model. Today they expect a three-year bottoms-up build with scenario analysis. The bar moved because AI tools make the excuse of 'we are too early for financial modelling' transparently false."

Beyond Elevation's fractional CFOs prepare founders for investor due diligence at pre-seed by building the full financial pack before the raise starts — not in response to an investor's request during the round. The difference in closing speed is measurable.

## When Should a Pre-Seed Company Upgrade From Fractional to Full-Time CFO?

A pre-seed company should upgrade from a fractional CFO to a full-time hire when monthly finance operations consistently exceed 80 hours. That threshold typically arrives between Series A and Series B, around £3M to £5M in annual recurring revenue.

Until that point, fractional delivers more value per pound. A full-time hire at pre-seed or seed will be under-utilised and over-compensated relative to the work available. The goal of a fractional CFO engagement is to build the finance function well enough that the eventual full-time hire inherits a system — not a blank page.

Hayat Amin's rule for the handoff is specific: when you are spending more on ad hoc CFO hours than a full-time salary would cost, and when reporting, investor relations, board governance and operational finance each demand dedicated weekly attention, the fractional model has done its job. That is the signal to hire — not before.

## FAQ

### Is a fractional CFO the same as a part-time accountant?

No. An accountant records transactions and files returns. A fractional CFO builds financial strategy, models, investor materials and tax-efficient structures. The roles overlap in exactly zero places that matter at pre-seed.

### Can a pre-seed startup qualify for SEIS without a CFO?

Technically yes, but SEIS eligibility has structural requirements that most accountants do not proactively manage. A fractional CFO ensures the company qualifies before shares are issued — not after an investor's tax relief claim is rejected by HMRC.

### How many hours per month does a pre-seed fractional CFO work?

Typically 15 to 25 hours per month. During an active fundraise, this may spike to 30 to 40 hours for two to three months as investor queries and due diligence responses accelerate.

### What is the difference between a fractional CFO and a finance consultant?

A finance consultant advises. A fractional CFO executes. Beyond Elevation's fractional CFOs sit inside the company's systems, build the models, attend investor meetings and own the financial output. A consultant writes a report and leaves.

### Do I need a fractional CFO if I am bootstrapping?

If you plan to raise within 12 months, yes. If you are permanently bootstrapped, the case is weaker — but tax structure, R&D credit positioning and financial reporting hygiene still justify a lightweight engagement of 8 to 12 hours per month.
