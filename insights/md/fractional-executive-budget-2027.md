# Your 2027 Fractional Executive Budget: Three Operators for Less Than One Full-Time Hire

The average Series A company budgets £180K for a full-time CFO it will not hire until Q3. According to a 2026 Kruze Consulting survey, 67% of venture-backed startups between $2M and $10M ARR have no full-time finance executive — yet 71% of those same companies budget for one every year. Hayat Amin argues this is the most expensive line item founders never spend, because the budget locks up capital that could fund three fractional operators delivering results from week one.

A fractional executive budget for 2027 should not be a discounted version of a full-time hiring plan. It should be a different document entirely — one that allocates spend by function delivered, not by headcount assumed.

Beyond Elevation places three types of fractional operator — CFO, Chief IP Officer, and AI Operations lead — and the companies that budget correctly for all three spend less in total than the ones that budget for a single full-time hire they never make. Here is the framework that separates a wasted budget from a productive one.

## Why Do Most 2027 Executive Budgets Still Assume Full-Time Hires?

Most executive budgets assume full-time hires because board templates and investor expectations were designed in an era when fractional executive work did not exist at the C-suite level. The result is a budget with a £150K–£250K line item for a CFO, nothing allocated for IP strategy or AI operations, and a hiring timeline that pushes the actual start date past the point where the work was needed.

The fundamental error is budgeting by role rather than by output. A full-time CFO costs £150K–£250K in salary plus benefits, equity, and recruitment fees. The recruitment process alone takes three to six months. By the time the hire starts, two quarterly closes have passed without a senior finance operator in the room.

Fractional executives eliminate this gap. A fractional CFO at £4K–£8K per month starts in January, not July. A fractional Chief IP Officer at £3K–£6K per month files the patents your general counsel has been sitting on since October. A fractional AI Operations lead at £5K–£9K per month automates the reporting pipeline your finance team builds manually every month.

The 2027 fractional executive budget should reflect what the company needs built by March, not what org chart it hopes to fill by December.

## What Does a Fractional Executive Budget Actually Look Like in 2027?

A fractional executive budget allocates between £12K and £23K per month for a full fractional C-suite — roughly £144K to £276K per year. That range covers a CFO, a Chief IP Officer, and an AI Operations lead at current 2027 market rates. The floor of that range costs less than a single mid-market CFO salary, and it delivers three distinct functions instead of one.

Here are the numbers Beyond Elevation sees across its client base in late 2026:

**Fractional CFO:** £4,000–£8,000 per month. Two to three days per week. Covers month-end close, board reporting, investor updates, cash flow forecasting, and fundraising preparation. Companies under £5M revenue sit at the lower end. Companies between £5M and £20M with complex cap tables or multi-entity structures sit at the upper end.

**Fractional Chief IP Officer:** £3,000–£6,000 per month. One to two days per week. Covers patent audit, filing roadmap, trade secret classification, licensing strategy, and pre-fundraise IP positioning. Hayat Amin's rule for IP budgeting is direct: if your company owns more than three patents or generates more than £2M in revenue from a defensible product, you are spending more money not having a CIPO than the CIPO costs.

**Fractional AI Operations Lead:** £5,000–£9,000 per month. Two to three days per week. Covers process automation audit, agent rollout, back-office AI implementation, and AI governance framework. This is the newest fractional role and the one with the widest rate range because the talent pool is still forming.

The combined annual cost for all three ranges from £144K to £276K. A single full-time CFO with recruitment fees, benefits, and equity dilution runs £180K to £350K. The maths is not close.

## How Do You Decide Which Fractional Roles to Budget For?

Budget for the function that is currently costing you the most money by its absence — not the role that sounds most senior on an org chart. Hayat Amin's Executive Budget Stress Test ranks the three fractional functions by a single metric: what did the company lose in the last 90 days because nobody was running this function?

If you missed a fundraise window because your numbers were not investor-ready, the CFO function comes first. If a competitor filed a patent on something your team built eighteen months ago, the CIPO function comes first. If your finance team spends 40 hours per month on reporting that an AI agent could do in four, the AI Operations function comes first.

For most companies between £2M and £15M in revenue, the right sequence is:

**Stage 1 (£2M–£5M):** Start with a fractional CFO. The finance function is the first to break under growth pressure and the first investors ask about.

**Stage 2 (£5M–£10M):** Add AI Operations. By this stage, the back office consumes enough labour to justify automation, and the CFO needs clean, real-time data to make decisions.

**Stage 3 (£7M–£15M):** Add a Chief IP Officer. At this revenue level, the company generates enough IP to protect and enough revenue to license. Waiting longer means prior art accumulates and competitors file first.

The stages overlap deliberately. Hayat Amin reminds founders that the worst fractional executive budget is the one that funds zero fractional executives and waits for a full-time hire that never materialises. A £4K per month fractional CFO who starts in January outperforms a £180K full-time CFO who starts in September — every time.

## What Is the 90-Day ROI Test for a Fractional Executive?

A fractional executive should deliver measurable ROI within 90 days of engagement start. If the engagement costs £6K per month, the first quarter costs £18K. The fractional should have produced at least one outcome worth more than £18K — a closed fundraise, a reduced month-end close cycle, a patent filed that blocks a competitor, or an automated process that eliminates a planned hire.

Beyond Elevation tracks three ROI markers across its fractional placements:

**Time recovered.** How many hours per month did the fractional save the founder or existing team? At most growth-stage companies, a fractional CFO saves the CEO 15–20 hours per month on finance tasks the CEO was handling personally. At a founder-equivalent hourly rate, that alone covers the engagement cost.

**Revenue unlocked.** Did the fractional directly enable revenue that would not have happened? A fractional CIPO who structures a licensing deal or positions IP for a fundraise creates revenue that would have been zero without the role.

**Risk eliminated.** What exposure did the fractional close? A trade secret left unclassified, a patent window left open, a month-end close running 15 days late — each is a compounding liability. The fractional's value is not just what it generates. It is what it prevents.

If a fractional executive cannot point to a concrete outcome worth more than their fees after 90 days, the engagement is misconfigured. The issue is almost never the operator — it is the scope. ## How Should You Structure Q1 2027 to Maximise Fractional Impact?

The first quarter of a fractional engagement sets the trajectory for the entire year. In one recent engagement, Hayat Amin showed how a company that hired all three fractional operators on the same January start date produced an investor-ready board pack, a patent filing roadmap, and an automated month-end close within 11 weeks. That company raised a Series A three months later at a valuation 30% above comparable companies without those assets in place.

Three rules for structuring Q1:

**Rule 1: Start before January.** The best time to sign a fractional engagement is November or December. The operator uses December for a diagnostic audit, and January starts with execution rather than orientation. Companies that wait until January to begin the search lose the entire first quarter to onboarding.

**Rule 2: Define deliverables, not hours.** Budget the engagement by output, not by time on site. A fractional CFO who delivers a two-day close process in 10 hours per week is more valuable than one who works 20 hours per week but takes eight days to close. [The deal structure determines the engagement's ceiling.](/insights/fractional-executive-deal-structure/)

**Rule 3: Run all three functions in parallel.** A fractional CFO, CIPO, and AI Operations lead share context. The CFO needs the IP valuation to build the fundraise model. The CIPO needs the financial data to price licensing deals. The AI Operations lead needs both to know which processes to automate first. Running them sequentially wastes the information overlap that makes the fractional model work.

Your 2027 fractional executive budget is a capital allocation decision, not a headcount forecast. The companies that treat it as a function-delivery plan — three fractional operators starting in January instead of one full-time executive starting in September — will have better numbers, stronger IP, and faster operations by the time the full-time-hire companies finish their recruitment process.

## FAQ

### How much should a startup budget for fractional executives in 2027?

Between £12K and £23K per month for a full fractional C-suite covering CFO, Chief IP Officer, and AI Operations. A single fractional CFO costs £4K–£8K per month. The total is typically less than the all-in cost of one full-time senior hire.

### Can a company hire more than one fractional executive at the same time?

Yes, and the best-performing companies do. Running a fractional CFO alongside a fractional CIPO and AI Operations lead creates shared context that makes each role more effective. The combined cost is still lower than a single full-time CFO plus recruitment fees and equity.

### When should a company switch from fractional to full-time?

When the function requires more than three days per week of continuous attention and the company can sustain a £150K-plus salary without compromising runway. Most companies between £2M and £15M in revenue never reach this threshold for all three functions simultaneously.

### What is the biggest mistake companies make when budgeting for fractional executives?

Budgeting by headcount instead of by function. Allocating £180K for a full-time CFO that takes six months to hire wastes both the budget and the time. Allocating the same £180K across three fractional operators delivers results from week one.
