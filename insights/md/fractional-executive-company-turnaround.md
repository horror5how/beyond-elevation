## What Is a Fractional Executive Turnaround?

A fractional executive turnaround is a time-boxed engagement — typically 90 to 180 days — where an experienced C-suite operator joins a distressed company to stabilise cash flow, restructure operations, and position the business for recovery or exit. The operator works inside the company, not from a consultancy office. They own decisions, sign off on payments, and report directly to the board.

This is not advisory. The fractional executive runs the function. They have signing authority. They attend creditor calls. They rebuild the cash forecast from scratch.

The model works because turnarounds need speed and judgment, not more analysis. A company burning £200K a month cannot wait 6 weeks for a restructuring firm to finish its diagnostic phase. The fractional executive starts by triaging cash — the same day.

## Why Are Distressed Companies Choosing Fractional Executives Over Restructuring Firms?

Distressed companies choose fractional executives over restructuring firms because the cost-to-speed ratio is not close. A mid-market restructuring engagement from a Big 4 firm runs £150K to £350K in fees alone, with a 4 to 8 week mobilisation period. A fractional executive starts at £2,500 to £4,000 per day and is operational inside a week. Over a 90-day engagement, total cost typically falls between £50K and £100K — a third of the advisory alternative.

The speed gap matters more than the cost gap. Hayat Amin's rule for turnaround engagements is direct: if the company has fewer than 120 days of cash, every week of delay reduces the survival probability by 7 to 9%. Restructuring firms run on consulting timelines. Fractional executives run on operating timelines.

There is a second structural advantage. Restructuring firms produce recommendations. Fractional executives execute them. The company does not need a report that says "cut headcount by 15% and renegotiate the lease." It needs someone who has already done both — who picks up the phone, calls the landlord, and negotiates the break clause by Friday.

## What Does a Fractional Executive Do in the First 30 Days of a Turnaround?

In the first 30 days, a fractional executive runs a hard triage: stop the cash bleed, identify the 3 survival levers with the highest impact, and rebuild the operating rhythm so the board has visibility by week 2. The first 30 days determine whether the company stabilises or slides into administration. Beyond Elevation's turnaround engagements follow a structured sequence Hayat Amin calls the 90-Day Turnaround Sprint.

**Days 1 to 7 — Cash X-Ray.** The fractional executive rebuilds the [13-week cash flow forecast](/insights/cash-flow-forecast-13-week-model/) from bank statements, not from the company's projections. Most distressed companies discover their actual runway is 3 to 6 weeks shorter than they reported to the board. The X-Ray identifies the three largest weekly cash drains and ranks every discretionary payment by survival impact.

**Days 8 to 14 — Revenue Triage.** Which revenue lines are profitable? Which customers are worth saving? Which contracts should be renegotiated or exited? The fractional executive runs a contribution margin analysis at the product or service line level and kills unprofitable work within the first two weeks.

**Days 15 to 21 — Cost Surgery.** Not across-the-board cuts. Targeted surgery. The fractional executive identifies the 20% of costs that drive 80% of the bleed — and removes or renegotiates them. This includes supplier renegotiations, lease breaks, contractor reductions, and the difficult headcount conversations most boards avoid.

**Days 22 to 30 — Stakeholder Alignment.** The board, investors, and senior creditors need a single document: what happened, what changed, and what the next 60 days look like. The fractional executive writes this deck — not a consultant. This is the document that determines whether stakeholders fund the bridge or walk away.

## How Much Does a Fractional Executive Turnaround Cost Compared to a Restructuring Firm?

A fractional executive turnaround costs 65 to 80% less than an equivalent restructuring firm engagement. The real numbers for a mid-market company with £5M to £30M in revenue in 2026 are stark.

**Restructuring firm (Big 4 or mid-tier):** £150K to £350K for a 90-day engagement. Mobilisation takes 4 to 8 weeks. The firm assigns a partner at 10 hours per month, a director, and 2 to 3 analysts. The deliverable is a report and a recommended plan. Execution is the company's problem.

**Fractional executive turnaround:** £50K to £100K for 90 days at 3 to 4 days per week. Operational in 5 to 10 days. One experienced operator with full signing authority. The deliverable is the turnaround itself — executed, not recommended.

Hayat Amin reminds founders that the cost comparison misses the real gap: "A restructuring firm bills to diagnose. A fractional executive bills to fix. The diagnosis takes two weeks because the operator has already seen this pattern 20 times."

## When Is a Fractional Executive the Wrong Choice for a Turnaround?

A fractional executive is the wrong choice when the company needs formal insolvency proceedings, not operational rescue. If the company is past the point of trading solvency — meaning it cannot pay debts as they fall due and the directors face wrongful-trading liability — the first call should be to a licensed insolvency practitioner, not a fractional operator.

Three other scenarios where the model breaks:

**The board will not grant authority.** A fractional executive turnaround only works if the operator has signing authority and decision rights. If the founder or board insists on approving every decision, the speed advantage disappears. Hayat Amin's position is unambiguous: "If I cannot sign a £10K payment without a committee, I cannot stop a £200K monthly bleed."

**The company needs more than one function rebuilt.** A single fractional executive handles one function at operator depth — typically finance, operations, or IP. If three functions are simultaneously failing, the engagement needs a [fractional executive team](/insights/fractional-executive-deal-structure/), not one operator. Beyond Elevation builds these teams for exactly this scenario.

**The distress is market-structural, not operational.** If the business model is broken — the market moved, the product is obsolete, the unit economics never worked — no turnaround operator fixes that. The company needs a pivot, an acqui-hire, or a controlled wind-down. An honest fractional executive says this in week 1, not week 12.

## FAQ

### How quickly can a fractional executive start a turnaround engagement?

Most fractional turnaround executives are operational within 5 to 10 business days. Beyond Elevation maintains a bench of operators who have run turnarounds before — they do not need onboarding for distressed situations. The first deliverable is typically a 13-week cash flow forecast delivered within the first week.

### Can a fractional executive replace a restructuring firm entirely?

In most cases, yes — for operational turnarounds. A fractional executive handles cash management, cost restructuring, creditor negotiation, and stakeholder communication. The exception is formal insolvency, which requires a licensed insolvency practitioner by law. For pre-insolvency turnarounds, a fractional executive delivers more at lower cost.

### What authority does a fractional turnaround executive need?

The fractional executive should have full signing authority for payments up to a board-agreed threshold, hiring and firing authority within their function, and direct access to the board. Without decision-making power, the speed advantage — which is the entire point — disappears.

### How long does a typical fractional executive turnaround take?

Most turnaround engagements run 90 to 180 days. The first 30 days are intensive at 4 to 5 days per week. Days 30 to 90 shift to 3 days per week as the stabilisation takes hold. Beyond day 90, the engagement either transitions to a steady-state fractional arrangement or the function is handed to a permanent hire.
