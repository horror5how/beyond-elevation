# How to Learn AI for Small Business: Seven Free Courses and a 30 Day Plan on Your Own Work

The fastest way to learn AI for a small business is to use it on one real job in your own company. Take one free course of under ten hours, then spend four weeks applying it to a task you've timed. You don't need to code, and you don't need a certificate.

Hayat Amin spent twenty years as a technology chief financial officer and sold three companies, and he now builds AI operations inside companies himself. The plan below is what we'd tell an owner who wants to learn this without hiring anyone yet. Every course and figure was read on the provider's own page on 5 October 2026.

## why most owners are stuck at the start

Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses Voices surveyed 1,256 owners from all 50 states between 27 January and 4 February 2026. 76 percent said they use AI and 93 percent of those said it had helped. Only 14 percent said it was fully embedded in their core operations, and 73 percent said more training and implementation resources would help. The barriers they named were a lack of technical expertise, difficulty choosing tools, and data privacy.

That survey is of Goldman's own programme participants, so it runs high. The Census Bureau's wider read is lower. A Census working paper published in April 2026 found 18 percent of US firms used AI in a business function between November 2025 and January 2026. The most common uses were sales and marketing at 52 percent, strategy at 45 percent and IT at 41 percent. 57 percent of the firms using it had it in three functions or fewer.

Most owners have already opened ChatGPT or Claude, so access isn't the issue. Turning a chat window into a changed process is the skill that's missing, and it's one you pick up by doing it once.

## what you need to learn, and what you can skip

You need four things: how to describe a task so a model does it well, how to check what it gives back, which jobs to hand over and which to keep, and where your data is allowed to go. Anthropic's free small business course is built around exactly those four, what it calls delegation, description, discernment and diligence.

You can skip Python, neural networks and machine learning maths. They matter if you're building models. You're not. If a course asks you to write code in week one, it's for someone else.

## the free courses worth your hours

We'd take one of the first two and ignore the rest until week four. There are seven here because owners ask about all of them.

- **AI Fluency for Small Businesses, Anthropic Academy.** Free, with a quiz and a certificate. It covers how AI works, its limits, keeping a human in the loop and being open with customers about AI use, with examples from customer service, back office, supply chain and leadership. It's the one written for your situation.

- **AI For Everyone, Andrew Ng on Coursera.** About 7 hours across 4 modules, free to audit, and 2,626,172 people had enrolled when we checked. It explains what AI can and can't do for a company, with no code. The certificate costs money, the learning doesn't.

- **Google AI Essentials on Coursera.** Under 5 hours in 5 modules, with prompting and productivity exercises. It's 49 dollars a month after a 7 day free trial in the US, so finish it inside the trial if you can.

- **Elements of AI, University of Helsinki and MinnaLearn.** Free, and more than 2 million people have signed up. Part 1 needs no maths or programming. Part 2 recommends basic Python, so stop after Part 1.

- **OpenAI Academy.** Workshops, livestreams and recorded sessions, with a small business community and in person ChatGPT for small businesses events, one of them in Houston on 21 September 2026. Good for seeing someone else's screen.

- **Your local Small Business Development Center.** Nearly 1,000 centres run by the SBA's resource partner network, offering no cost advising. America's SBDC says more than 400 of its advisors are AI certified and have helped over 8,000 small businesses. This is the only option here where a person looks at your own business.

- **The US Chamber of Commerce training built with Google.** Free AI training for small business, listed in the Chamber's March 2026 guide next to Grow with Google's Make AI Work for You video series.

## a 30 day plan that uses your own work

Week one. Finish one course, then write down ten jobs your team repeats every week. Put a rough time and a count next to each one, for example 40 quotes a week at 15 minutes each, which is 10 hours. If you can't put a number on a job, you can't tell later whether AI helped.

Week two. Pick the job with the most hours and the fewest judgement calls. Write the instructions you'd give a new hire on their first day, paste them into Claude or ChatGPT with three real past examples, and run it on five jobs. Don't paste customer personal data until you've read the provider's business terms.

Week three. Run it side by side with the person who does the job today. They do it the normal way, the model does it too, and you compare 20 outputs. Count how many you'd send without a change. Rewrite the instructions wherever the misses cluster.

Week four. Time the new version. If 10 hours became 4, you've learned AI the way that matters, and you have a number to show for it. Then pick job two from your list. The St. Louis Fed found workers who used generative AI saved 5.4 percent of their hours, about 2.2 hours a week, but across everyone it was 1.4 percent. The difference is whether someone changed the process or only opened the app.

## Frequently asked questions

### How to learn AI for small business?

Take one free course of under ten hours, such as Anthropic's AI Fluency for Small Businesses or Andrew Ng's AI For Everyone, then apply it to one timed job in your company for four weeks. You'll learn more from 20 side by side comparisons on your own work than from a second course.

### How can I use AI for my small business?

Start with a repeated written task, such as quotes, follow up emails or supplier invoice checks. Give the model the instructions you'd give a new hire plus three past examples, and check its output against your own for a week before you trust it.

### How to use Claude AI for small business?

Use it the same way you'd brief a new member of staff. Write the task out in full, attach real examples, and ask it to say when it isn't sure. Anthropic's free AI Fluency for Small Businesses course teaches this, including when to keep a human checking the result.

### How to implement AI in small business?

One process at a time, timed before and after. Pick the job with the most hours and the fewest judgement calls, run it side by side with the person who does it now, and only roll it out once you'd send most outputs unchanged.

### Can AI help small businesses?

Yes, when it changes a process rather than sitting in a browser tab. In Goldman Sachs's 2026 survey, 93 percent of owners using AI said it had a positive impact, but only 14 percent had it fully embedded in core operations.

### What is AI automation for small business?

It's software that does a repeated job without a person keying each step, with a model reading the parts a rule can't, such as an email or a PDF invoice. Learning to prompt is step one. Automation is the step after, once the job crosses two or more systems.
