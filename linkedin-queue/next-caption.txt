Most AI agents create work, not remove it.

The agent sends 50 outbound emails. Your SDR reviews all 50.

The agent generates 15 contract summaries. Your lawyer checks all 15.

That is not automation. That is a junior assistant who never sleeps.

Real ROI from agentic AI requires a gate.

Gate 1: agent acts autonomously below a confidence threshold
Gate 2: human reviews the 5% that hits the flag
Gate 3: every flagged case trains the gate higher

Stripe's fraud model doesn't review every transaction. It surfaces only the ones that cross the threshold. 80% fewer human reviews. Better outcomes.

Build the gate first. Then scale the agent.
