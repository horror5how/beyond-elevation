Your revenue grew 40% last quarter. You almost ran out of cash. That is overtrading. Most founders have never heard of it.

More revenue does not mean more cash.

Three things eat your working capital as you grow: longer debtor days, more stock, slower supplier payments.

A fractional CFO spots this in week one. Most founders only see it after the bank call.

The fix: a 13-week rolling cash flow, tightened payment terms, and a credit facility before you need it.
