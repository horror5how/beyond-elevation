73% of startup enterprise value is intangible.

Fewer than 15% have run a formal IP assessment.

10-signal diagnostic. Takes 10 minutes.

5 signals you have leverage:
– Competitors need 18+ months to replicate
– Data compounds the product over time
– Know-how lives only in your team's heads
– Rivals are filing patents around your space
– Technology works in 3+ adjacent markets

5 signals you are exposed:
– Copyleft code in the stack
– Published before filing
– Claims are narrow or feature-level
– No IP assignment with early contractors
– Innovation stops at product, not process

Run this before due diligence does.
