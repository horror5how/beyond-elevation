Q4 starts today. Your board wants 5 metrics. Most management teams can produce 2.

Net Revenue Retention tells investors if your product works. 110%+ means customers expand before you sell more.

Burn Multiple tells them if your growth is fundable. Above 1.5x and each pound of new ARR costs more than it earns to generate.

Customer concentration is the number most founders hide. One client over 30% of revenue kills institutional LOIs before they start.

The three your accountant never flags: gross margin by product line, cash conversion score, IP revenue as its own line.

A fractional CFO builds this pack in week one. Your bookkeeper builds a P&L.
