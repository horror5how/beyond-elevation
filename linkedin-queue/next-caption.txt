Most founders lose 20% of their exit value in the EBITDA reconciliation.

Add-backs are upward adjustments acquirers accept before pricing you.

Most founders do not know what qualifies.

Acquirers do.

5 add-backs your fractional CFO should load into your data room today:

Founder salary above market rate.

One-time legal and advisory fees.

Non-recurring product launch spend.

Audit-readiness and IPO-prep costs.

R&D expensed but capitalizable under IFRS.

Average uplift: £340K per deal.

That is 22% of reported EBITDA sitting unclaimed.

Your acquirer is not going to flag it for you.
