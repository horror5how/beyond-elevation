Most founders think they have a revenue problem.

It is a collections problem.

Your invoices go out 30 days late.
Your terms say net-30 but your average collection is net-90.
Your cash position is 60 days behind your P&L.

A 5-day improvement in your collection cycle is worth more than a 10% revenue increase at your margin.

Nobody told you that because nobody measured it.

Fix the cash cycle before you fix the growth rate.
