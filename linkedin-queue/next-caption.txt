Most companies hire a fractional exec. Nobody tells them how to manage one.

3 months in, the engagement drifts. The work is fine. But there's no direction. £36,000 later, the CEO can't name one deliverable.

Fractional execs aren't consultants. You can't hand them a brief and walk away.

The ones that work have: one mandate, one owner, 90-day output targets.

The ones that fail have: multiple stakeholders, vague scope, no KPIs.

Before the next fractional hire, write one sentence: what does success look like at day 90?

If you can't write it, you're not ready to hire.
