Two companies. Same revenue. Same headcount. One exits at 3x. The other at 8x.

The difference is three numbers most accountants never flag.

Customer concentration: if your top customer is more than 30% of revenue, the acquirer discounts the multiple.

Net revenue retention: 120% NRR gets a premium. 85% NRR gets a question mark. The gap is whether existing customers spend more each year.

Revenue predictability: £3M recurring beats £5M project every time.

Most founders learn this during diligence. By then it is too late to fix.

Your CFO should know this before the process starts.
