A fractional Chief IP Officer is different.

In the first 12 months, one typically:

— Finds 3–6 IP assets not on your balance sheet
— Structures 1–2 licensing conversations worth £500K–£2M
— Kills 20–35% of your patent spend on dead filings
— Documents monetisation that adds 0.4–0.8x to your exit multiple

Monthly cost: £4,000–£8,000.

One term sheet change covers the year.

The question is not whether you can afford a fractional CIPO.

It is what you have already left on the table.
