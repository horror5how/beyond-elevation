Two companies. Same revenue. Same headcount. One exits at 3.2x. The other at 8.6x.

The difference is not the product.

It is what they own.

Businesses with documented IP, royalty income, and data licences exit at 2.4x the multiple of identical businesses without.

Most founders cannot name their intangible assets in a data room.

That is the valuation gap. It takes 18 months to close, not 18 days.
