Most finance teams take 8 days to close the month.

The best ones do it in 2.

Not harder work. Different wiring.

Day 1: automation runs while the month is still open.
Day 2: human review on a clean ledger.

3 things that unlock a 2-day close:
Revenue recognised at transaction, not month-end.
Bank rec runs on Day 28, not Day 31.
Variance commentary auto-drafted from actuals vs budget.

Faster close means cleaner numbers.
Cleaner numbers mean a better exit.

If your close still takes a week, the problem is wiring, not effort.
