Most founders file a provisional patent and move on.

12 months later the priority date has expired.

Here's what that costs you.

The provisional buys 12 months of "patent pending" for ~£1,500.

Don't convert by month 12 and that date is gone forever.

A competitor can file the same invention the next day and claim it first.

Three things nobody tells you:

1. The deadline is non-extendable. No exceptions.
2. File PCT at month 11 to get 30 months of international runway.
3. Tell your IP counsel the deadline before your Series A — not after.

We've seen acquirers walk away. The invention was real. The protection was not.
