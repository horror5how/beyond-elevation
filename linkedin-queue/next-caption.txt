Q4 due diligence opens in 8 weeks.

Acquirers request 3 IP documents in the first 48 hours.

Document 1: Patent assignment chain. Every inventor. Every contractor. Every co-founder. One gap voids the claim.

Document 2: Active licensing ledger. Running royalties, minimums, sublicense rights. Missing this = 0 IP revenue to underwrite.

Document 3: IP valuation report (income approach). Not your lawyer's estimate. An independent one.

73% of founders don't have all three ready.

That gap costs 25-40% of deal value before the NDA is signed.
