When your fractional CFO leaves, they take 18 months of institutional knowledge with them.

Unless they hand it over properly.

Most don't.

The average fractional engagement lasts 8 months.

The average handover: 1 exit meeting and a shared folder.

That gap costs 6 months of rebuild time.

What a proper handover looks like:

A living finance manual, not a Notion dump.

Documented model assumptions, not just outputs.

30-day successor overlap, not a Friday email.

The fractional model only works if the exit is planned from day one.
