Your P&L shows £340K profit.

Your bank shows £18K.

That gap has a name: debtor days.

The average UK B2B company waits 54 days to get paid.

You invoice on day 1. Cash lands on day 54.

Meanwhile: payroll goes out on day 28. VAT on day 37.

A CFO models this gap before it opens. An accountant explains it after.

If your receivables are over £100K and you have never built a 13-week cash forecast, you need a CFO.
