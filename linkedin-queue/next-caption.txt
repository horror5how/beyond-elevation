73% of CFOs have never put a number on their company's data.

It's probably their second-biggest asset.

Data doesn't depreciate. It compounds.

Every query, transaction, and customer signal your business generates has a reuse value your balance sheet ignores.

Three ways data creates real financial value:
- Licensing it to third parties (Reddit earns $203M/yr)
- Using it to train proprietary models your competitors can't replicate
- Pledging it as collateral for non-dilutive capital

A CFO who can't value data can't defend the balance sheet in 2026.
