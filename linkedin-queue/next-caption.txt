The average SME spends £47K a year prosecuting patents.

Average licensing revenue: £0.

That's not an IP problem. That's a commercial structure problem.

Filing a patent creates a right.

Filing a licensing framework creates a revenue line.

Most founders know how to do the first one. Almost none have done the second.

3 things that kill licensing before it starts:

No rate card. No minimum guarantee clause. No commercial owner for the IP.

Your IP counsel drafts claims. They don't build licensing programmes.

That's a Chief IP Officer job. And most companies don't have one.
