Acquirers spend 90 days in your data room. Most founders give them 9 years of unsorted mess.

The average M&A deal loses 23% of its headline price in due diligence.

Not because the business is bad.

Because the CFO started exit prep 6 months before the banker call, not 18.

Month 1–3: clean 3-year P&L, normalise EBITDA, document every add-back.

Month 4–9: IP title chain audited, recurring revenue separated from one-offs, customer concentration below 25%.

Month 10–18: management accounts investor-ready, data room built, CIM drafted.

Acquirers pay a premium for clean books. They discount for noise.
