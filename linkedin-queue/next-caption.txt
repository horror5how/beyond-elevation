Every investor at Series A asks for an IP schedule.

73% of founders don't have one.

It delays the round by 6 weeks.

Your IP schedule is 5 items: what you own, where it's registered, who created it, whether assignment agreements are signed, any licences granted.

Most founders have none of this documented.

The investor doesn't care about the IP. They care that you do.

A Chief IP Officer builds this in a week. Due diligence takes 6.
