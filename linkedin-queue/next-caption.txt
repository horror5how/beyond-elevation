Most founders file patents.

None of them build a licensing financial model.

That's why 80% of patent programs generate zero revenue.

The model has 3 inputs:

Royalty base: total licensable market, not your revenue

Rate: industry benchmark plus a strategic premium

Escalator: contractual step-up at each renewal term

One tech founder ran the numbers. First deal closed with a $2.3M minimum guarantee.

Acquirers model your licensing program at due diligence.

Build the model before they do.
