Q4 due diligence asks one question most AI companies cannot answer.

Who owns what you built this year?

The answer is not in your contracts. Not in your patent filings. Not in your data agreements.

Three IP items to document before December:

1. Model weights: fine-tuned on your data, but your vendor contract may say they own the output.

2. AI-generated code: in production since Q2, with no IP assignment clause in the contractor agreement.

3. Proprietary dataset: fed to 3 external tools, none of which have confidentiality carve-outs.

A Chief IP Officer documents this in 30 days. Without one, you discover it in the deal room.
