Hire a fractional CFO. Here is what they do in the first 100 days.

Week 1: audit what exists. Bank access, accounting software, last 3 years of accounts.

Week 2: build a 13-week rolling cash forecast. Not projections. Real cash timing.

Month 2: find the top 3 revenue leaks. Most SMEs lose 18% of revenue to process gaps.

Month 3: one dashboard, three KPIs. Every decision runs through it.

CFOs who spend 100 days in meetings are consultants in disguise.
