Your CFO tracks 40 financial ratios.

None of them are about IP.

Three that belong in every board deck:

IP Investment Intensity: IP spend / revenue. Under 1% = you're underprotected. Benchmark: 2-5%.

IP Monetization Ratio: licensing income / gross IP asset value. Under 8% = capital is sitting idle.

IP ROI: licensing revenue / prosecution + maintenance cost. Under 3:1 = the portfolio doesn't pay for itself.

73% of IP portfolios are systematically underfunded by all three.

Companies that track them raise at 2.4x higher multiples.
