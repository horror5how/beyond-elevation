The average IP licensing deal takes 9 months from first contact to signed agreement.

Most founders budget 3.

Phase 1 (months 1–3): identify and qualify licensees. Most founders skip straight to outreach.

Phase 2 (months 4–6): negotiate terms. 60% of deals stall here because the founder never set a walk-away rate.

Phase 3 (months 7–9): legal review and execution. Budget $15–40K in external counsel.

The founder who plans this in Q1 closes revenue in Q4. The founder who starts in Q3 is still negotiating at year-end.
