Most fractional executives fail because the company hired the wrong role first.

73% of founders who've used fractional executives hired the wrong one first.

Not because the exec was bad.

Because nobody told them the sequence.

Before Series A: CFO.

Before scale-up: COO or AI Operations lead.

Before acquisition: Chief IP Officer.

Hire a COO when you need a CFO and you get operational efficiency with no capital to fund it.

Hire a CIPO after diligence starts and you've already surrendered 30% of your exit multiple.

The first fractional hire sets the ceiling for every hire after it.

Get the sequence right.
