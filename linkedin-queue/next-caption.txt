Most founders automate the broken process.

Not the optimised one.

AI makes a bad process 10x faster. It doesn't fix it.

Before deploying any agent: map the process on paper.

Find the 3 steps that only exist because of a legacy system or habit.

Cut them first. Then automate.

Founders who run a 2-week process audit before automation cut AI failures by 60%.

The ones who skip it spend £80K on a tool that creates new problems.
