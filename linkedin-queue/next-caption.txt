Your AI model has an 18-month shelf life.

Your training data does not.

GPT-3. GPT-4. GPT-4o. Each version makes the last one worthless to license.

The data that trained it? Every new model needs the same type.

Reddit signed a 10-year data deal with Google. No AI model carries a 10-year price tag.

Model access deals run 12–18 months before superseded.

Training data licenses run 5–10 years with annual escalators.

Founders building on model access are building on a depreciating asset.

Founders who own training data are building a perpetual annuity.

That distinction does not show in your pitch deck. It shows in your exit multiple.
