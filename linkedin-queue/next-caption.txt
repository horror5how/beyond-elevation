Most £3M businesses are paying for the wrong finance hire.

An accountant records what happened. A controller keeps the books. A fractional CFO changes what happens next.

The difference is not seniority. It is the question they ask.

Accountant: "What did we spend?"
Controller: "Does it reconcile?"
Fractional CFO: "Where are you bleeding margin?"

At £2M–10M ARR, you need the third question. You do not need to hire for it full-time.

Full-time CFO: £180K+ salary. Fractional CFO: £25–45K per year.

Same question. Different cost structure.
