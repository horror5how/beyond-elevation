In M&A, the IP floor gets set in the first 48 hours.

After that, it never moves up.

Acquirers run 4 checks before they open your financial model.

Chain of title. One patent still in a founder's name cuts the deal 15% minimum.

Licensing revenue. Defensive-only IP gets discounted 30-40%. One active licence changes the floor.

Claim breadth. Your attorney wrote for grant. Acquirers price for enforcement. Most portfolios fail this test.

FTO exposure. They check if you're infringing something yourself. Most founders haven't.

Fix these before the bankers arrive, not after.
