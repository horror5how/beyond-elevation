Physical AI companies move hardware in 18 months. US patents take 42.

You cannot patent a robot before your competitor ships the next generation.

The founders winning protect three layers in this order:

Software claims first — fastest to grant, covers the core algorithm.

Fleet telemetry as a trade secret — nobody else has it.

Hardware patents last — pending status deters infringers while you ship.

Figure AI raised at $2.6B. Three IP layers, not one patent.

Most founders file one hardware patent and wait. By grant, the category moved.
