3 in 4 US patents generate zero licensing revenue in year 1.

Not because the patents are weak.

Because the licensee learned the founder wasn't watching.

The 90-day window after grant is when your royalty floor gets set — or it doesn't.

First 30 days: Send formal notice to known infringers. Start the damages clock.

Days 31–60: Mark your products. Every unmarked unit is a day of lost damages.

Days 61–90: File a licensing offer — even one refusal creates a market price.

Most founders spend those 90 days on product. That's how you stay at $0.
