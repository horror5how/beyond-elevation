Most founders sell their IP for a check.

The smarter play: contribute it for equity.

A $2M patent portfolio contributed to a joint venture returns a 20% equity stake.

You keep the asset. You own the upside. Zero dilution.

Three tech companies did this in H1 2026. All three are holding stakes worth more than the cash a sale would have paid.

IP contribution for equity is the most underused capital structure in tech.

If your patents are idle, they are a currency nobody is spending.
