Filing a patent for your AI feature takes 3 years and $50K.

Your competitor copies it in 90 days.

The math does not work.

There is a free alternative that blocks competitors from patenting your idea.

It is called a defensive publication.

One document, published through the USPTO Prior Art Database.

Zero cost. 24 hours. Instant prior art.

Three times this beats filing a patent:

1. Feature with a 6-month shelf life — publish, block, move on

2. Methods you cannot enforce — make them public so nobody else can own them

3. Speed beats a 3-year patent timeline

When to still patent: foundational infrastructure taking 18 months to copy.
