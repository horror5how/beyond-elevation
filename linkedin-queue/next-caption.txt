Two founders co-developed an AI tool. Filed the patent together. Then the enterprise client started licensing it to their competitors — and owed nothing.

Joint patent ownership is a trap most founders never see coming.

Under US law, any joint owner can license the patent independently and keep 100% of royalties.

No consent from you. No revenue share to you.

Fix this before your next co-development deal:

Demand sole assignment in every contract.

Add a licensing consent clause and revenue split if joint ownership is unavoidable.

Audit chain of title before any financing — joint ownership is the #1 red flag acquirers find.
