Your month-end close takes 8 days. The best fractional CFOs do it in 2.

Not because they work faster.

Because they replaced the manual work with AI agents.

Agent 1: pulls actuals from the ERP at close-of-day on the 1st. No waiting for the team.

Agent 2: runs variance analysis against budget. Flags every line over 5% with a reason code.

Agent 3: drafts the board narrative. CFO reviews, edits 3 paragraphs, done.

The 8-day close is a process problem. Not a headcount problem.
