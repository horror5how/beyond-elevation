70% of startup patents earn £0 in licensing.

Not because patents don't work.

Because most are filed wrong.

Wrong scope: claims too narrow to license or enforce.

Wrong timing: filed pre-PMF, abandoned before product ships.

Wrong type: utility patent on a surface a design patent covers in 12 months at one-fifth the cost.

Three corrections fix this: right type for the surface, right timing for the stage, continuation strategy from day one.

A 3-patent cluster with those corrections beats five standalone filings on licensing revenue.
