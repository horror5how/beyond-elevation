73% of AI companies have no IP portability clause in their foundation model contract.

Switch providers and your fine-tuned weights may belong to the old one.

3 clauses every AI API agreement needs before you sign:
→ Model output ownership (you vs. provider)
→ Fine-tune portability and reversion rights
→ Derived training data ownership on migration

Companies that skip these spend $1.8M average untangling IP ownership after a forced switch.

Your exit multiple drops when the IP stack is tied to a single vendor.
