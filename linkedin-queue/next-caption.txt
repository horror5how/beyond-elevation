Most AI companies cannot define their unit economics.

Investors terminate term sheets because of this.

SaaS unit economics do not work for AI.

Token costs are variable. Models depreciate. Inference gets cheaper — then obsolete.

3 numbers you need before your next raise:

1. Fully-loaded gross margin — include every dollar of compute

2. CAC payback corrected for model costs — when does your AI product actually pay back?

3. Net revenue retention adjusted for model depreciation

68% of AI Series B founders answer these wrong in due diligence.

The ones who answer correctly raise at 2.4x higher multiples.
