Your IP counsel renewed 67 patents last quarter. None were assessed for licensing potential.

That's not an IP function. That's a cost centre.

The Chief IP Officer job description most boards write has it backwards.

They hire to protect. A real CIPO is hired to monetise.

3 questions every CIPO asks before renewing any patent:
Who would pay to license this?
What's the royalty window before expiry?
What does protection cost vs what it earns?

73% of patents earn zero licensing revenue in their lifetime.

Not because the IP has no value. Because no one owns the extraction job.
