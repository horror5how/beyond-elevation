Most companies still have a full-time C-suite. That is a legacy cost.

One full-time CFO salary buys four fractional executives covering finance, IP, AI operations, and general management.

Deploy all four in 14 days. Not 6 months.

No notice period. No NI contributions. No equity dilution.

The full-time C-suite was built for an era when there was no other option.

There is now.
