Your IP strategy is probably a cost line. It should be a revenue line.

IP monetisation is the process of turning patents, data, and trade secrets into recurring income.

Most founders have never heard the term. They file patents to protect their product. Not to generate cash from licensing, royalties, or asset sales.

A medtech founder had 3 patents sitting idle for 4 years. Maintenance fees going out. Zero revenue coming in.

18 months after an IP monetisation review: £220K annual licensing income. Pure margin. No new product launched.

73% of UK SMEs with registered IP generate £0 from it.

That is a cash flow problem with a known solution.
