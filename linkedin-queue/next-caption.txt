Most founders have never had a CFO audit their pricing.

The gap between what you charge and what you could charge is 8–12 margin points.

4 places a good CFO looks first:

1. Discounting — 68% of deals close below list price with no pushback.

2. Contract terms — payment milestones costing 3–4 weeks of cash.

3. Tiering — most SaaS pricing has 3 tiers but 60% of revenue in one.

4. Cost-to-serve — the client you think is profitable often isn't.

A CFO who only closes the books hasn't done their job.
