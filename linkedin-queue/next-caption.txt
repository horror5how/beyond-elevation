Your competitor filed 23 patents last quarter.

You filed 0.

That is not a legal problem.

That is a valuation problem.

Patent velocity is a signal VC and acquirers use to price defensibility.

3 filings a quarter: table stakes.
10 a quarter: you have a moat.
0: you are building on sand.

A Chief IP Officer watches competitor filings weekly and translates them into your strategy.

Your lawyer does not do this. That is not their brief.
