Acquirers are finding them. Investors aren't.

Asset 2: Data collected in normal operations. Never valued. Never licensed. Worth more than the product in some deals.

Asset 3: Processes patented by employees who left. Nobody checked the assignment agreement.

Three weeks of IP hygiene before Q4 diligence. Six-figure difference at close.
