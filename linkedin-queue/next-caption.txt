Qualcomm earns $5.6B a year without making a single chip.

It doesn't have one IP asset. It has a 3-tier stack.

Tier 1: Patents — the public face. Licensees see these. Courts enforce these.

Tier 2: Know-how — the hidden lever. The implementation guide your patent doesn't include. The negotiation asset most founders never price.

Tier 3: Trade secrets — the moat under the moat. The stuff that dies the moment you disclose it wrong.

Most founders patent at Tier 1 and leave Tier 2 and Tier 3 unmonetized.

Qualcomm doesn't. Ericsson doesn't. Nokia doesn't.

3 assets. 1 stack. $5.6B/year.
