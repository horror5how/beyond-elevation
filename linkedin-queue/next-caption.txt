Your board wants 3 scenarios for 2027.

Most founders send 1.

The difference between base, upside, and downside is not a spreadsheet exercise.

It tells your board how well you understand your own business.

A fractional CFO builds the scenario architecture:

Revenue tied to 2 key assumptions, not 7.

Headcount phased against milestones, not calendar months.

Cash floor defined for each scenario.

Board decision triggers written in advance.

Your accountant files last year's numbers. A CFO models the ones that decide next year.
