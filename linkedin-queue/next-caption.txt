The standard royalty rate for a software patent licence is 4–6% of licensee revenue.

The average founder-negotiated rate is under 0.8%.

That gap is not a negotiation problem.

It is a valuation and strategy failure.

IP lawyers price your patent's validity.

IP strategists price its leverage.

A 4% rate on £5M licensee revenue = £200,000 per year per licence.

At 0.8%, that is £40,000.

Same patent. Same claim. Different preparation.

Three things drive royalty rates up: documented market impact, prior art map, enforcement credibility.

Most founders have none of them written down before the first licence negotiation.
