42 days to year-end.

Most founders have 3 IP deadlines passing quietly in December.

They find out in January. It costs 10x to fix.

4 IP moves before year-end:

1. Renewal audit. Any patent, trademark or design right due in 90 days?

2. Chain of title check. Does your company own what it thinks it owns? One unsigned contractor agreement breaks this.

3. Licensing review. Minimum guarantees. Notice periods. December is when these trigger.

4. Trade secret log. What know-how did your team share with AI tools this year? Courts ruled it unprotectable.

November is 3 hours of work.

January is a 6-figure repricing in due diligence.
