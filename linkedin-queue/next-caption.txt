Your accountant added £340K back to your EBITDA. Your acquirer rejected £280K of it.

Here's what sticks in diligence — and what doesn't.

Add-backs acquirers accept:

Founder salary above benchmarked market rate. If documented.

One-off legal fees for a resolved dispute. With the invoice.

Capex reclassified as opex under £50K. If auditable.

Add-backs acquirers reject:

"Management time." Every time.

Normalisation adjustments without third-party support.

Related-party transactions at off-market rates.

At 6.5x EBITDA, that £280K rejection costs £1.82m at exit.

Fix your add-back schedule before you appoint advisers.
