Your lawyers protect IP. A Chief IP Officer turns it into income.

Most founders conflate the two.

A lawyer files. A lawyer defends. A lawyer bills by the hour.

A Chief IP Officer asks: which of these assets can I put a recurring revenue line against?

Companies with active IP licensing programmes trade at 2.3x the EBITDA multiple of companies without one.

Most founders are paying for protection. They're not getting paid by the IP.

That changes when you stop managing IP as a legal cost and start running it as a commercial function.
