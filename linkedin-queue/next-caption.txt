Most companies treat their IP as a cost centre.

The acquirer sees a revenue line.

That gap is your valuation discount.

Three things that shift IP from cost to revenue:

File patents with licensing terms in mind, not just protection.

Audit which competitors are infringing before your next raise.

Price each renewal fee against expected licensing yield, not the attorney quote.

One company patents to protect.

The other patents to earn.
