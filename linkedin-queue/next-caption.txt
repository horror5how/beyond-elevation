Your fractional CFO has been with you 3 months. You still don't know if they're working.

Most founders measure fractional executives by hours. Wrong metric.

3 numbers that tell you if the retainer is actually delivering:

Days to close. Should drop 40% within 60 days.

Investor-ready data room. Should exist within 90 days.

CFO cost per £1M raised. Should be under £8K.

If none of these moved, you hired a part-time employee. Not an operator.
