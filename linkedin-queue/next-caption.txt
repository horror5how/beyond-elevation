Patent licensing firms take an average of 40% of every deal they close for you.

Most only engage when your portfolio has a realistic $2M+ licensing opportunity.

They work on contingency — which means they prioritise the deals that pay them fastest, not yours.

Average time from signing to first cheque: 18 months.

Know your numbers before you sign a 3-year exclusivity agreement.

The right firm for $500K in royalties is not the right firm for $5M.
