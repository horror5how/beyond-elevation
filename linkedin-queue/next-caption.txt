You built a SaaS product. Your contracts give the customer full data ownership.

You just handed away your most valuable asset.

Most founders sign standard templates. The data clause looks fine. It is not.

Anonymised aggregate data is worth licensing. You cannot use it if your contract says it belongs to them.

Three clauses to fix now:

1. Data ownership vs licence: who holds title to raw inputs
2. Derived data rights: who owns insights built from that data
3. Anonymisation carve-outs: whether you can use aggregate data at all

Fix this before the next deal. Not in due diligence.
