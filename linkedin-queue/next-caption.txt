Most founders know their revenue. Acquirers don't look at revenue first.

The 6 numbers they pull before anything else:

1. Gross margin (not revenue — margin)
2. Customer concentration (top 3 = what % of ARR?)
3. Net revenue retention (are you losing customers quietly?)
4. Payroll as % of revenue
5. Days Sales Outstanding
6. Rolling 12-month EBITDA, not annual

The founders who exit at 7x understood these numbers before their banker arrived.

The ones who exit at 3x scrambled to explain them in the data room.
