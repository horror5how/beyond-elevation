An NDA does not protect your IP.

73% of UK startups have NDAs with contractors.

Fewer than 20% have properly executed IP assignments.

Different documents. Different legal effects.

An NDA stops your contractor from disclosing.

An IP assignment means YOU legally own what they built.

Without the assignment, they own the IP.

4 situations where this has killed exits:

1. Contractor dispute pre-Series A
2. Missing chain of title in due diligence
3. Patent attribution rejected at prosecution
4. Acquirer walks on IP ownership gap

Costs nothing to fix before you sign.

Costs everything to fix after the fact.
