Most companies build AI agents when they need AI copilots.

That's a 6-month deployment failure.

Copilot: AI suggests, human decides. Production in 4-6 weeks.

Agent: AI decides and acts autonomously. Minimum 3-6 months. Needs rollback, audit trail, error budgets.

When you need a copilot: first AI deployment, customer-facing workflows, regulated processes.

When you need an agent: proven process, defined decision tree, low error consequence.

Companies that confuse the two spend 3x more to build the wrong thing.

Architecture first. Tool selection second.
