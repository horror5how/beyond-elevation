Most founders build a finance function backwards.

They hire a bookkeeper. Then an accountant. Eventually, a CFO.

By the time the CFO arrives, there are years of inconsistent data to clean.

The right order: process first. Tools second. People third.

A CFO-designed finance function closes the books in 2 days, not 8.

It produces board-ready numbers before every meeting. Not 2 weeks before a raise.

63% of UK founders define the finance headcount before they define what the function should do.

The ones who design first raise at higher multiples and exit cleaner.
