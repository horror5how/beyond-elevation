Your AI agent was 94% accurate on day 1.

You deployed it. Moved on. Never checked it again.

6 months later it is still running. And costing you more than it saves.

3 things every agent needs every 30 days:

Accuracy check. Sample 50 outputs. If the error rate exceeds 3%, reprompt.

Cost audit. Token spend compounds. What costs £0.12 per task today is often £0.40 by month six.

Handoff review. The agent passes work to a human somewhere. That handoff breaks silently.

Most founders deploy AI agents and read the uptime dashboard.

Nobody checks whether the agent still does what it was built to do.
