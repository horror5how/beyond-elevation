Most founders hire an interim executive when they need a fractional one.

Interim is for a crisis. £1,500/day. 3–6 months. Exits when the fire's out.

Fractional is a permanent capability at £4,500/month. 18 months average. Builds the function and stays.

One solves a problem that already happened. The other stops it happening again.

The crisis costs you the same either way. Only one of them makes the function better.
