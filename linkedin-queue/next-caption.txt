A European patent used to take 17 separate lawsuits to enforce.

Since June 2023, the UPC changed that to one.

The UPC issues provisional injunctions in 48-72 hours.

That is not a litigation strategy.

That is a licensing negotiation tool.

A licensee who knows you can shut them out of 17 EU markets in 72 hours takes your call differently.

Most founders sit on EU patents they have never used to close a licensing deal.

The Unified Patent Court was built for founders who treat IP as revenue, not paperwork.
