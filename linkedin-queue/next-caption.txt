A full-time Chief IP Officer costs $300K all-in. Most founders hire a patent attorney instead.

The attorney writes patents. The CIPO turns them into revenue.

Those are not the same job.

Full-time hire: $300K, 6 months to recruit, 12-month equity cliff before they ship a strategy.

Fractional: $3,500/month, live in 2 weeks, first IP revenue audit done in 90 days.

97% of companies with granted patents have never collected a single licensing payment.

That is not a patent problem. It is a management problem.
