Most founders send their patent attorney to licensing negotiations.

That's the single biggest reason deals underprice by 30%.

Licensing is a commercial conversation, not a legal one.

Your attorney defends the claims. They don't know your revenue model, your market positioning, or how a licensee calculates value.

The commercial negotiator closes the deal. The attorney reviews the contract.

Most founders never separate the two roles.

The ones who exit at 4x+ do.
