Most founders have IP worth licensing. Zero have a first deal.

Not because the IP has no value.

Because nobody told them what a first deal looks like.

$50k upfront. 4% royalty. 3 years. That is a licensing deal.

You do not need a law firm. You need four clauses:

1. What they are licensed to do

2. The minimum royalty ($25k to $150k is normal)

3. Your audit right

4. Termination if they stop paying

Your IP is not speculative. It is unlicensed.
