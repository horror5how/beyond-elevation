Most founders hire a fractional CFO without asking a single operational question.

"Walk me through your last month-end close."

"Name a cash flow problem you fixed in the last 12 months."

"Can I call a founder you've worked with?"

If they hesitate on any of these, you have a consultant. Not a CFO.
