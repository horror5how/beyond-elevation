Most founders sign 2-year license deals.

Strategic buyers price IP over 10.

Residual term is the single biggest variable in how acquirers DCF your royalty stream.

A 2-year deal with no renewal: worth ~1.7x annual royalty.

A 10-year evergreen with escalation: worth ~5.8x the same royalty.

Three clauses that close the gap:

Auto-renewal unless 90-day written notice.

CPI escalator tied to inflation, not performance.

Sub-licensing limited to affiliates only.

You're not changing the rate. You're extending the duration acquirers price.

The deal you sign today is the deal they value at exit.
