You outsourced development to save £180,000.

That developer may own your product.

Most founder contracts with overseas devs have no IP assignment clause.

Your acquirer's lawyer finds it on day 1 of due diligence.

The deal pauses. You discount 15% to cover the risk.

The fix: a 2-page IP assignment agreement, signed before line 1 of code.

If nobody owns this in your company, you need a Chief IP Officer.
