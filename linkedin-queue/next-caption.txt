Your gross margin is lying to you.

Most SaaS founders report 68% gross margin to investors.

Their true contribution margin is 34%.

That gap costs 2.4x at exit.

Gross margin excludes customer success, onboarding, and implementation costs.

Contribution margin includes every variable cost tied to delivering revenue.

PE buyers and strategic acquirers model on contribution margins — not gross.

If your unit economics look broken at contribution level, the deal dies in diligence.

Fix the reporting 3 months before your raise, not after.
