Most founders don't know their gross profit per customer.

Their acquirer will find it in 48 hours.

Investors don't price revenue. They price margin per unit of customer.

If you can't answer 'what does it cost to serve your best customer?' you are not exit-ready.

3 numbers separate a fundable pitch from a great sales deck: gross profit per customer, payback period, NRR.

Your fractional CFO should have all three on a single slide before Q1 starts.
