Your contractor delivered the code last week.

You paid the invoice. You don't own what they built.

Work created by a contractor defaults to them — not you — in most jurisdictions.

73% of founder contracts have no IP assignment clause.

That gap costs £840K to resolve at Series B diligence. Investors find it. Deals fall apart.

One fix: add three lines to every contractor agreement before work starts.

"All IP created is assigned to [Company] upon creation. Contractor waives moral rights. This clause survives termination."

No solicitor required. Done in 20 minutes.
