I've reviewed 200+ patent applications.

Here are 4 situations where I always say: don't file.

Filing costs £8,000–25,000 before prosecution. 83% of patents never generate £1 in licensing.

Filing without a commercialisation plan is a cost centre.

The 4 situations where filing is the wrong call:

1. You have 1 patent and no licensing programme to exploit it

2. Your market moves faster than patent pendency (18–30 months)

3. You can't enforce it — defendants sit in jurisdictions you can't afford to litigate

4. A trade secret gives better protection with zero public disclosure

Build the IP asset first. Then decide how to protect it.
