Your burn rate doesn't tell investors what they need to know.

Your burn multiple does.

Burn rate: how fast you're spending.

Burn multiple: dollars burned per dollar of new ARR.

Under 1.5x: you're building a machine.

Above 3x: VCs pass. Quietly.

73% of pre-Series A founders can't calculate theirs on the spot.

Your fractional CFO should flag it every month before your investors ask.
