News Corp got $70M for its archives. Reddit got $65M from Google. Most founders holding equivalent proprietary data have priced it at $0.

The AI training data market is $1.8B in 2026 and growing 40% a year.

Your floor isn't zero. Your floor is what the last comparable dataset sold for — minus negotiating discount.

3 steps to value yours:

1. Map the data type (text, images, structured, time-series)
2. Find the last 3 closed licensing deals in that category
3. Apply a volume discount for size, a freshness premium for recency

The founders who skip this sell equity. The ones who don't sell a license.
