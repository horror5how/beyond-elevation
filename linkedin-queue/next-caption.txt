Your acquirer will request 36 months of management accounts. Most SMEs can produce 12. Maybe.

Q4 is when the gap between your finance function and your ambitions becomes visible.

Three things investors check before they look at your product:

Management accounts that reconcile to bank statements

A 13-week rolling cash forecast — not a spreadsheet

Revenue split by customer, not just by product line

Get these wrong and you lose 20–30% of your exit multiple before negotiations start.

The fix is not a better accountant. It is a function that runs on process, not people.
