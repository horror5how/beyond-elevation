Most AI agents break not because the code is wrong.

They break because nobody designed the handoff.

8 in 10 AI agent deployments add a human review step in month 2.
The teams that skip it have a 3x higher failure rate by month 6.

The pattern: an agent handles 85% of cases automatically.
The other 15% needs a human. Without a clear handoff, the agent either halts or guesses.

Both are expensive.

An AI operations function designs the handoff before the agent ships.
Not after it breaks.
