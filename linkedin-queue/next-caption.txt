In 2019 you had 3 years to file before your competitor did.

In 2026 you have 6 months.

AI patent filings at the USPTO doubled between 2022 and 2025.

The first-mover window didn't shrink because the technology changed.

It shrank because 50,000 more companies entered the same queue.

File a provisional before you ship or spend $200K fighting prior art later.

3 things compressing your window: faster model iteration, more competitors filing, faster examiner rejection cycles.

1 thing extends it: a provisional on launch day.
