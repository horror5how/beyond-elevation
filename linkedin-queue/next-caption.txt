Your revenue grew 40% last year. Your bank account shrank.

That is the working capital trap.

When sales outpace collections, growth destroys cash.

3 signs you're in it:
- DSO crept past 45 days while you weren't watching
- Creditors paid in 30, customers paying you in 68
- Board wants 18-month runway. You can only show 9

Most founders hire another salesperson to fix it.

The fix is a cash conversion cycle audit, not a new AE.

A fractional CFO identifies the gap in week one and closes it in 90 days. Without adding headcount.

Revenue growth with no cash to show is just a faster way to run out.
