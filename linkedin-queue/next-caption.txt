Your board just approved a £5M acquisition.

Nobody asked about the IP.

3 questions every board should put on the agenda before any major deal:

1. Is every IP asset formally assigned — employees, contractors, acquired companies?

2. Are there open-source or AI-generated contamination risks?

3. What licensing revenue has this IP generated, and what is it leaving on the table?

IP is 68% of average enterprise value.

Most boards cannot name one IP asset they own.

That is why the Chief IP Officer exists.
