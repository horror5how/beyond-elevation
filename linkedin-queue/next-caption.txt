Most founders think their cashflow problem is a revenue problem.

It's not.

The average growth-stage company has £340K of working capital sitting idle in 3 places.

Collections: invoices 60+ days overdue that nobody is chasing.

Accruals: expenses booked wrong quarter, distorting the P&L.

AP timing: paying 30-day terms when suppliers accept 90.

A fractional CFO finds these in 30 days.

No new revenue. No cuts.

Just better use of what's already there.
