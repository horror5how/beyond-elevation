Most founders have not updated their hire-or-not decision since.

That is not a rounding error. That is a full junior hire's worth of extra cost every year.

A fractional CFO pays no employer NI. No pension contribution. No redundancy liability.

The maths changed in April 2025. Most boards are still running 2024 numbers.
