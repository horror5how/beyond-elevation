Most scale-ups hire their first full-time CFO 18 months too early.

A full-time hire at director level costs £150,000 all in.

A fractional CFO delivers the same output for £2,000–£5,000 a month.

Same board pack. Same investor reporting. Same exit prep.

The difference: you're not paying for the days they're doing nothing.

Fractional makes sense until you hit £5M revenue and need someone in the building daily.

Most companies are nowhere near that threshold when they hire full-time.
