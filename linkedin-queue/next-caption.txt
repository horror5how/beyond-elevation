Data licensing without a minimum guarantee is charity.

Reddit negotiated $203M per year from Google.

Not projected. Contractually guaranteed.

3 clauses that turn a data deal into a revenue line:

1. Annual minimum — a floor, or you walk

2. True-up audit rights — quarterly, not annually

3. Volume escalator — auto-renegotiate at defined thresholds

Founders skip all three.

Then they call $12K/year "licensing revenue" on the pitch deck.

The minimum guarantee is what makes data licensing bankable.

Without it, you have permission. Not revenue.
