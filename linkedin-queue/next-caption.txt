3 in 5 fractional CFOs fail in their first quarter.

Not because they're bad.

Because the brief was wrong.

The founder gave them: 'sort out the accounts.'

The CFO needed: cap table, debt structure, next milestone, investor timeline.

Most fractional CFO engagements fail at context, not execution.

A proper brief has three things:

Goal. Fundraise, exit prep, cashflow, compliance — not 'fix finances.'

Constraints. What's off-limits. What the board agreed.

Pressure. Who needs what, by when.

Right brief: delivers in weeks. Wrong brief: first month gone figuring out the job.

That's your Q4 window.
