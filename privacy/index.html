Privacy

What this site knows about you.

What we collect. This site uses PostHog analytics to count visits, see which pages get read, and track which buttons get clicked. That means your IP address, browser type, pages viewed and clicks. No forms exist on this site, so we collect nothing else here.

Why. To learn which pages convince and which confuse, so the site gets better. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Booking a call. The booking link goes to our scheduling provider. What you enter there (name, email, company) is used to run the call and follow up, nothing else.

Email and phone. If you email or call us, we keep the correspondence. We do not add you to any list without asking.

Opting out. Add ?notrack=1 to any page URL and this browser is excluded from analytics from then on.

Your rights. Ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it: clara.hawkins@beyondelevation.com. We answer within 30 days.
